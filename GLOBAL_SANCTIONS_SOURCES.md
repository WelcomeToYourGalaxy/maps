# Global sanctions and designation lists — sources

Source catalogue for `global_sanctions.html`.

The map carries **94,563 mapped listings** drawn from **94 official designation lists**
issued by national governments, the EU and the UN Security Council. Lists are aggregated
through OpenSanctions, which pulls each one from its issuing authority's own published file.
No entry is added, inferred or edited here.

A further **13,082** designated targets publish neither an address nor a country —
mostly crypto wallets, securities and aircraft — and cannot be placed on a map.

## Location precision

Each point is labelled in the popup with how precisely it is placed.

| Precision | Listings | What it means |
|---|---:|---|
| Street address | 7,605 | The list publishes a street address and it geocodes to that address. |
| City / locality | 44,117 | The list publishes a city or locality; the point sits at the city centre. |
| Country only | 42,841 | The list publishes only a country; the point sits at the country centre. |

A city-level or country-level point is not the entity's address. Street-level points come
from the geocoded address set built for the original OFAC map; city and country points are
resolved against the GeoNames gazetteer.
