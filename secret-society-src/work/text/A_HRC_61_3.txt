I. Introduction
1. Special procedures are mandated by the Human Rights Council to report to it on their
activities (see annex).
2. In 2009, the sixteenth annual meeting of special rapporteurs, independent experts and
chairs of working groups decided that a joint communications report would be prepared
(A/HRC/12/47, paras 24-26), with this decision being reconfirmed by the seventeenth annual
meeting in 2010. This was to avoid duplication, rationalize documentation, allow
examination of cross cutting issues, and ensure that the content of communications and any
follow-up would feed into the universal periodic review mechanism more effectively.
Mandate holders decided that the report should contain summaries of communications and
statistical information (A/HRC/15/44, para. 26-27).
3. The outcome of the review of the work and functioning of the Human Rights Council
calls on the Office of the United Nations High Commissioner for Human Rights to maintain
information on special procedures in a comprehensive and easily accessible manner and
encourages the use of modern information technology to reduce the circulation of paper
(resolution 16/21, annex, paras. 29 and 60).
II. Communications sent and replies received
4. All communications and their replies are available in their entirety through the special
procedure communications website at the following link: Home Page. This includes short
summaries of all allegations communicated to States or other entities.
5. This report covers all urgent appeals, letters of allegation, and other letters sent by
special procedures mandate holders between 1 June and 30 November 2025 and replies
received from 1 August 2025 until 31 January 2026. Communications sent before 1 June
2025 are reported in previous communications reports submitted to the Council and also
made available on the dedicated website: Communications reports of special procedures |
OHCHR .
6. This report also includes replies received from 1 August 2025 until 31 January 2026
relating to communications sent by special procedures mandate holders before 1 June 2025.
Some of these replies supplement information communicated earlier by the respective States
or other entities. Link to replies is available at Replies received
7. The present report contains urgent appeals sent by the Working Group on Arbitrary
Detention and the Working Group on Enforced or Involuntary Disappearances and joint
urgent appeals sent by them with other mandates. It does not contain other types of
communications issued by these mandates, which are processed according to their own
distinctive procedures and are reported separately in the annual reports of these two working
groups.
8. The names of some alleged victims have been redacted to protect their privacy and to
prevent further victimization. Names of victims are mentioned only when the individual
concerned or their direct family has expressly consented or requested to have his, her or their
name in the public report. In the original communications, the full names of the alleged
victims were provided to the Government concerned after obtaining their appropriate
consent. Names of alleged perpetrators have systematically been redacted in communications
and State replies to preserve the presumption of innocence.
