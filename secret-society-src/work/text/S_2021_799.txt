Enclosure
Report to the United Nations on Kosovo Force operations
1. This report is submitted in accordance with Security Council resolution 1244
(1999) and covers Kosovo Force (KFOR) activities during the period from 1 April to
30 June 2021.
2. As at 30 June 2021, the total number of KFOR troops in theatre remained
approximately 3,400, from 20 North Atlantic Treaty Organization (NATO) and eight
non-NATO troop-contributing nations.
3. There were no major security incidents during the reporting period and no
request for KFOR intervention as a third security responder.
Security situation update and operations
4. During the reporting period, there was no significant change to the overall
security situation in Kosovo, which remains generally calm but fragile, owing to
unresolved political and interethnic issues. There were no impediments to the safe
and secure environment and freedom of movement.
5. On 4 April 2021, Vjosa Osmani, was elected by the Assembly of Kosovo,
replacing Hashim Thaçi. On 15 June, Ms. Osmani confirmed the extension of the
current mandate of the European Union Rule of Law Mission in Kosovo (EULEX)
until June 2023.
6. KFOR continued to conduct routine framework operations across the KFOR
area of operations, while maintaining a proactive and flexible force posture. Since the
outbreak of the coronavirus disease (COVID-19) pandemic, KFOR has maintained its
ability to conduct its day-to-day operations. At the same time, KFOR has provided
assistance to local communities and municipalities. KFOR has been fully
implementing the preventive measures recommended by the World Health
Organization for the protection of military personnel.
7. KFOR continued to maintain its close cooperation with other security providers
in the Balkans joint operations area.
8. There were no significant security incidents during the reporting period and
KFOR remained mission effective. Although activities were impacted by the
COVID-19 pandemic, KFOR successfully contributed to maintaining a safe and
secure environment and freedom of movement across the area of operations and was
prepared to act as a third security responder, if required.
Summary
9. During the reporting period, KFOR continued to fulfil its mandate under
Security Council resolution 1244 (1999) to maintain both a safe and secure
environment and freedom of movement as part of a comprehensive international
effort. The close coordination and cooperation at the tactical level between KFOR,
EULEX and the Kosovo police was effective.
