# Review pass: country and pattern writeups on the Infiltration Map

The map is about INFILTRATION: an outside party (foreign government or spy service, crime group, company/industry, bank, lodge/secret society, religious movement, elite network, armed movement, lender) getting its people, money or control INSIDE a government, international body or company so decisions serve it. That includes office-holders being members of lodges/orders/movements that work to place members in office, foreign states paying or recruiting officials, the revolving door from a firm into the post that regulates it, and criminals paying police, judges, customs or politicians.

Writers sometimes padded writeups with material that is NOT infiltration. Remove sentences about: hacking, spyware or phone surveillance from outside; disinformation or fake-account campaigns; abductions, deportations or "police stations" abroad aimed at diaspora; openly signed treaties, troop deployments, mercenary contracts, leases or stake purchases; citizenship/passport sales; family succession or a leader's own power grabs; banks/casinos fined for weak anti-money-laundering checks; drug seizures or trafficking routes with no official shown working for traffickers; products sold to criminals; lawful presence of an organisation with nothing shown.

Also: the writeup must describe the pattern, not list cases (max two named cases per country, three per pattern). Keep the voice: plain, grounded, novice-friendly, no purple prose, no punchline sentences. Do not add new facts. If cutting leaves a country with nothing on infiltration, write a short plain estimate (about 100–160 words) based on the Global Organized Crime Index description already in the text, and set "est": true. Keep paragraphs separated by "\n\n".

Edit only what needs editing; leave good text alone. Write the result to the given output path in the same shape as the input, and validate it parses. Reply with the names you changed and one line each on what you cut.
