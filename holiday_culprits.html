Who corporatized holidays

Although usually originating authentically, holiday momentum has largely become economic in nature or at least numb under it, the occasions not collective memories but commodification by commercial interests, their relationships almost entirely manufactured, no longer genuine celebration but consumption oftentimes suppressing the very qualities that they claim to honor.
