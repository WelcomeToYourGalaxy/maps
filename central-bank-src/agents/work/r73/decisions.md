# Round 73 decisions

## Kyrgyzstan stays on "exactly one". Both `other` events refused for re-kinding.

The removals agent proposed re-kinding `events_gold.json` idx 82 to a counted kind,
which would have moved Kyrgyzstan off the exactly-one list with no research at all.
Refused. Read both records together and the reason is the same for each:

- idx 82, 26 June 2024: parliament approved Kubanychbek Bokontaev's resignation
  letter and relieved him ahead of schedule. 24.kg gives no reason from either side.
- idx 92, 18 March 2026: Melis Turgunbaev submitted a resignation letter, announced
  by the speaker. 24.kg calls it voluntary and gives no reason.

Neither source states pressure. Every counted kind on the removals row asserts a
cause - dismissed, forced out, pushed to resign. Filing either of these under one
of those kinds would put a claim on the map that no source makes. This is the
Namibia precedent, already settled: an unexplained early exit is not pressure.

So Kyrgyzstan holds one counted record and will keep holding one until a source
states why either governor left. That is the correct number, not a gap.

## The `other` kind is doing two jobs and the handoff does not say so.

The handoff says `other` is for appointments and court rulings, and "never file a
real episode that way." Read on its own that reads as a ban, which is why an agent
looking at two Kyrgyz records filed `other` concluded they were mis-filed.

They were not. `other` holds two distinct things:

1. Events the layer deliberately excludes - appointments, routine court rulings.
2. Exits where no source states a cause, which cannot be put on the removals row
   without inventing the cause.

The warning only covers the first job. A documented episode of pressure must never
be hidden in `other` to dodge the work. An exit with no stated cause belongs there,
and moving it out is fabrication, not coverage.

Handoff wording to fix: say both jobs, and say that getting a record out of `other`
needs a source that states the cause, not a re-reading of the same source.
