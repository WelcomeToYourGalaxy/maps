## Why these thirteen are different, and why they may be the best value in the round
**Every one of these countries holds exactly one case record only because a UNION record is
expanded into it.** The seven WAEMU members share the 2011 BCEAO governor's resignation; the six
BEAC members share their union's record. Not one of them has a case of its own.

So a national finding here is effectively that country's first own record, and the route is the
NATIONAL audit institution, not the union's: each has a Cour des comptes or Chambre des comptes,
most publish in French, and several publish per-entity reports. Search the institution's own name
plus the national directorate of the central bank — the BCEAO and BEAC both run national
directorates in each member state, and a finding against a national directorate is a finding
against the central bank in that country.

**Do NOT file anything keyed to `BCEAO` or `BEAC`.** A union key expands to every member, so one
mistake puts a false record in seven to thirteen country boxes. Key it to the member country.

**And do not re-file the union record.** Read your country's `have_cases.json` entry: the record
already there is the union's, and offering it again for the member is a duplicate.
