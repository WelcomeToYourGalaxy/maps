1 documented case at the central bank:

2009-2015 · audit finding on dormant state accounts held at the central bank · official finding. Guyana's Auditor General, Deodat Sharma, reported in his 2013 report that eight accounts at the Bank of Guyana holding about 1.8 billion Guyana dollars had sat unused for more than five years, untouched since 2009. The individual balances he listed were 474 million for UK Programme Aid-GEA, 371 million for the Infrastructural Development Fund, 280 million for the Agriculture Rehabilitation Project, 213 million for SIMAP Phase II, 172 million for the Financial Sector Reform Programme, 127 million for Accountant General-GEC Wartsila, 77 million for an Agriculture Loan and 55 million for UK Programme Aid-Rice. The Audit Office had reviewed eleven special accounts at the Bank of Guyana with balances of about 4.3 billion dollars in total. The Auditor General said the money belonged in the Consolidated Fund and recommended the Ministry of Finance transfer it there as soon as possible, noting that on that basis the government's overall bank position would show a positive balance of 56 billion dollars. 2 documented career moves between the bank and private finance, the IMF or the World Bank:

Gobind Ganga (Governor): from Director of the Research Department of the Bank of Guyana to Alternate Executive Director at the World Bank in Washington, and back to the bank as Deputy Governor ; from Deputy Governor to Governor. The Bank of Guyana's governor profile page gives Gobind Ganga's full career. He was Director of the bank's Research Department from 1995 until November 2003, when he was appointed Alternate Executive Director at the World Bank in Washington, D.C.; he returned to the bank as Deputy Governor in June 2005 after that term ended, and became Governor on 2 December 2014. His earlier posts were academic, at the University of the West Indies at Mona in Jamaica, the Consortium Graduate School at Mona and the University of Guyana. No private employer appears anywhere in the career the bank publishes, and no part of it is in finance outside the public sector: the only outside institution is the World Bank. Governor's Profile, Bank of Guyana
Rajendra Rampersaud (Director at the Bank of Guyana): from Director at the Bank of Guyana (until 2018) to Director of Demerara Bank Limited. Demerara Bank Limited's own director profile page for Mr. Rajendra Rampersaud, published on its board-members listing dated 21 September 2022, states that he has over two decades of central banking experience and served as Director at the Bank of Guyana until 2018, holds master's degrees in monetary economics and finance from the University of Glasgow and in economics and planning from Moscow, and has contributed to the Inter-American Development Bank and the Caribbean Development Bank, and it lists him as a Director of the bank; the hiring employer is in finance and is private, and that ownership fact comes from Demerara Bank Limited's own audited Annual Report 2023, note 1 Incorporation and business activities, which states that the bank was incorporated on 20 January 1992 as a private limited liability company under the Companies Act Chapter 89:01 and was licensed to carry on the business of banking on 31 October 1994. PRESSURE IN PRACTICE

Government pressure reported in 0 of 37 quarters, 2010–2018.

1998-12-31 · statutory arrangement letting the government set the bank's exchange rate policy. Under section 29 of the Bank of Guyana Act, Chapter 85:02 (Act No. 19 of 1998, enacted 31 December 1998), the Minister, in consultation with the Bank, specifies the policies, principles and criteria of Guyana's exchange rate system, and the Bank is responsible only for the day-to-day administration of that system. Section 14(2) of the same Act lets the President terminate the appointment of the Governor, and the Minister terminate the appointment of any other Director, on stated grounds. Section 3(1) describes the Bank as an autonomous institution governed by the Act. This record rests on the consolidated text of the Act published by Guyana's Ministry of Legal Affairs. Laws of Guyana, Volume 16, Chapters 83.01-89.01: Bank of Guyana Act, Cap. 85:02 (Ministry of Legal Affairs, Guyana)
