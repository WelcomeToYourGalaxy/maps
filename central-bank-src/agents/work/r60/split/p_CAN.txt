The Bank of Canada issues the Canadian dollar, which moves within a band of 2% or less against the US dollar. Canada scores 21 of 100 on exposure to influence, where 0 means shielded and 100 means fully exposed; that is 181st of 194, among the least exposed. No documented case at the bank was found, and no government pressure was reported from 2010 to 2018. Pressure came later and from the opposition rather than the government: at a Conservative leadership debate on 11 May 2022, Pierre Poilievre pledged to fire governor Tiff Macklem if he became prime minister, and repeated it as party leader in December 2022. The Bank of Canada Act appoints a governor for seven years during good behaviour, and removing one would involve Parliament. The prime minister and the finance minister both defended the bank; the party's own finance critic was removed from that role after calling the pledge deeply troubling. The bank’s own profile of Mark Carney, governor from 1 February 2008 to 1 June 2013, describes a thirteen-year career at the investment bank Goldman Sachs, in its London, Tokyo, New York and Toronto offices, before he entered the public service. It is one of the 63 central banks that own the Bank for International Settlements. EY and PwC audit its accounts, and Canadian Bank Note Company and BA International print its notes. The Canadian dollar is 5.76% of world currency trading and 2.47% of foreign reserves, and the bank has 9 swap lines, which are agreements to borrow another central bank's currency.

1 documented case at the central bank:

2004 · internal controls / spending · official finding. Two 2004 internal audits of the Bank of Canada, released under the Access to Information Act, criticised the bank for slack financial practices. The audits found that employees of the central bank paid invoices without authorization, did not evaluate whether they got good value from suppliers, and violated a fail-safe system meant to guard against cheque fraud. One of the reports is dated 15 October. Audit scolds Bank of Canada (Central Banking)

PRESSURE IN PRACTICE

Government pressure reported in 0 of 37 quarters, 2010–2018.

2022-05-11 · public pressure. At a Conservative leadership debate in Edmonton on 11 May 2022, Pierre Poilievre, the front-runner and later party leader, pledged to fire Bank of Canada governor Tiff Macklem if he became prime minister, saying at the end of the debate: "I will fire the governor of the central bank to get inflation under control." He had called the bank "financially illiterate" the month before, and also promised to have it audited and to bar it from issuing a digital currency. Macklem had been appointed in 2020 to a seven-year term; the Bank of Canada Act says the governor is appointed for seven years "during good behaviour", and commentators noted that removing one would require Parliament's involvement. Prime Minister Justin Trudeau and Finance Minister Chrystia Freeland both publicly defended the bank's independence, and former governor David Dodge rejected the criticism. The party's own finance critic, Ed Fast, was removed from that role after saying publicly that the pledge was deeply troubling and hurt the party's economic credibility. Poilievre repeated the commitment as leader in December 2022. Frontrunner for Canada's Conservative leadership promises to fire central bank governor
