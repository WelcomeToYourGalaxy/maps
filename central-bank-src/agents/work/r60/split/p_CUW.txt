The Central Bank of Curacao and Sint Maarten issues the Caribbean guilder, pegged to the US dollar. It scores 50 of 100, where 0 means shielded from outside influence and 100 means fully exposed, more exposed than 95% of the 194 places scored. Neither independence index codes it. Crane Currency, headquartered in Massachusetts with plants in Malta and Sweden and a supplier to more than 50 central banks, was contracted to print the new banknotes, with the Royal Canadian Mint making the coins. One case is recorded. In July 2024 a court found former president Tromp and former deputy director Laurents guilty of document forgery over a backdated 2009 loan agreement under which over $400,000 went to Tromp's pension account. It acquitted them of official bribery and money laundering, including over a $1.5 million 2015 payment from the Venezuelan company Enpiso. One career move is documented: Bob Traa became president with effect from 13 June 2019, after working for the International Monetary Fund for over 34 years, and no private employer is named. That was a temporary appointment by the Supervisory Board, pending official appointment by Curacao and Sint Maarten.

PRESSURE IN PRACTICE

Not covered by Binder's 2010–2018 survey.

2017-05-26 · public pressure. A report published on 26 May 2017 said the Supervisory Board of the Central Bank of Curacao and Sint Maarten had voted three to two to extend a managing director's term, short of the supermajority required, and that the finance minister, Richard Gibson, had written to the board members Robert Pietersz, Tom Kok, Miriela Carolina, Vance Williams and Hubert Lopez asking them to work on a solution. The report quotes him saying that if a solution could not be found there would be no other choice but dismissal of the board. The report does not say who would carry out the dismissal. Impasse on extending term of Central Bank Managing Director (Curacao Chronicle, reprinted by Knipselkrant Curacao)
