ROYAL MONETARY AUTHORITY OF BHUTAN
Bhutan
Bhutan's central bank is the Royal Monetary Authority, which issues the ngultrum and keeps it pegged, or fixed, to the Indian rupee; the rupee circulates in the country as well. An anonymous informer raised a case in October 2014, and the final findings of Bhutan's Anti-Corruption Commission, covering 2014 to 2016, said a former deputy governor had allegedly forged official documents, misused power and concealed earnings from World Bank and Asian Development Bank projects. Those allegations are unproven; the case was forwarded to the Office of the Attorney General, with no later outcome. A currency swap with India, letting each side borrow up to 100 million US dollars, was signed on 17 March 2016 and ran out in March 2019. A commemorative 1,000-ngultrum note marking the Fourth King's seventieth birth anniversary was printed by De La Rue, not the ordinary circulating series, and the accounts for 2003 and 2004 were audited by M/s P.K. Mitra & Company. The bank scores 25 of 100, where 100 means fully exposed to outside influence.

PRESSURE IN PRACTICE

Not covered by Binder's 2010–2018 survey.

2010-06-02 · statutory arrangement letting the government direct the bank. The Royal Monetary Authority Act of Bhutan 2010, passed by Parliament on 2 June 2010 and in force from 22 June 2010, lets the executive direct the central bank: section 149 says the Royal Government may from time to time issue such directives to the Authority as it may consider necessary after consultation with the Governor, in the public interest, and must report them to Parliament. Of the Board's seven members, four are appointed by the Government and one of those must come from the Ministry of Finance. The Governor is appointed by the Druk Gyalpo on the Prime Minister's recommendation, and may be removed the same way, though only on a finding by a committee of permanent incapacity, incapability of carrying out the duties, or breach of the code of conduct. The Royal Government sets the Governor's salary and the board members' fees. The Act repealed the 1982 Royal Monetary Authority Act. This is a standing arrangement with no end date. The Royal Monetary Authority Act of Bhutan 2010 (Parliament of Bhutan)
