2026-05-04 · head of state reconstitutes the bank's board. President Mahmoud Abbas issued a presidential decree reconstituting the board of directors of the Palestine Monetary Authority, acting on a recommendation from the Cabinet and from the Authority's Governor. Three of the eight seats are held by virtue of law: the Governor as chairman, the Deputy Governor, and a representative of the Ministry of Finance, named as Majdi Al-Hassan.
