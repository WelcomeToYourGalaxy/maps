CENTRAL BANK OF DJIBOUTI
Djibouti
The Central Bank of Djibouti, created in 1977, runs what is called a currency board: the Djibouti franc is held at a fixed rate of 1 US dollar to 177.721 francs and is backed entirely by US dollars, so the bank cannot issue francs without dollars behind them. The franc itself was created in March 1949, outside the franc zone. The independence written into its law scores 0.698 of 1, where 1 means the bank holds the power rather than the government, and four reforms since 1970 have all raised that score. No documented case at the bank was found, which does not prove none happened. It scores 56 of 100, where 100 means fully exposed to outside influence, the second highest of the 194 places with a figure, with money-laundering risk in its banks rated 6.93 of 10, the thirteenth riskiest of 177. The country has had one systemic banking crisis since 1970. Its 2021 accounts were audited by Deloitte Côte d'Ivoire.

PRESSURE IN PRACTICE

Not covered by Binder's 2010–2018 survey.

2011-01-22 · statutory arrangement letting the executive appoint the bank's board. Under the statutes set by law no. 118/AN/11/6eme L of 22 January 2011, the Banque Centrale de Djibouti's board is made up of the Governor and six administrators appointed by decree, so every seat other than the Governor's is filled by the executive. The bank's own 2024 annual report states that composition, calls the board the bank's supreme organ and cites the 2011 law, which shows the arrangement still stands. This is a standing arrangement with no end date. The record rests on the bank's annual report, not on the text of the law, which could not be obtained.
