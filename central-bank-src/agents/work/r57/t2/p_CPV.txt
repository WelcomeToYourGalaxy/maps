===== BOX =====
BANK OF CAPE VERDE
Cape Verde
The Bank of Cape Verde issues the escudo, and the government of Portugal guarantees it under a currency cooperation agreement signed on 13 March 1998. The rate is fixed at 110.265 Cape Verdean escudos to the euro, having started at 0.55 escudos to the Portuguese escudo. The Portuguese Treasury provides a short-term credit line of 27.5 million euros, which can rise to 45 million, with drawings capped at 5 million, and Cabo Verde must transfer reserves equal to 35 per cent of what it asks for; Banco de Portugal states the facility obliges neither it nor any euro-area institution to step in. The bank has also signed a technical assistance agreement with the World Bank Treasury's Reserve Advisory and Management Partnership, becoming its 75th member, which is advisory work rather than management of its assets, and De La Rue printed the 1,000 and 2,000 escudo notes released on 15 April 2025. Its law scores 0.518 of 1 for independence, where 1 means the bank rather than the government holds the power. No case at the bank is documented, which is not proof none happened. It scores 47 of 100 for exposure to outside influence, ranking 12 of 194, with money issued, backed or fixed from abroad the main reason.

OWNERSHIP AND OUTSIDE CONTROL

Cabo Verde escudo – Portugal exchange rate cooperation agreement (external guarantee)

The Acordo de Cooperação Cambial was signed on 13 March 1998.
The peg was 0.55 CVE per Portuguese escudo and became CVE 110.265 per euro.
The Portuguese Treasury provides a short-term credit facility of EUR 27.5 million, expandable to EUR 45 million, with drawdowns capped at EUR 5 million; Cabo Verde must transfer reserves equal to 35% of the amount requested.
Banco de Portugal states the facility creates no obligation for Banco de Portugal or any Eurosystem institution to intervene.

Banco de Portugal, Revista de Estudos Económicos (April 2020) – Acordo de Cooperação Cambial entre Portugal e Cabo Verde
