NATIONAL BANK OF RWANDA
Rwanda
The National Bank of Rwanda issues the Rwandan franc, which in practice follows a crawling peg against the US dollar, meaning the rate is allowed to drift slowly in a managed way. It scores 27 of 100, where 0 means shielded from outside influence and 100 means fully exposed, and no government pressure was reported in the 37 quarters from 2010 to 2018. One case is recorded: the bank lost 63 million Rwandan francs from deposit accounts in early 2014 in a fraud reported to involve its own staff. Its director for information technology said the suspects had manipulated the bank's internal security system before taking the cash. One person was arrested and the file went to the Prosecutor General, while police said the main suspect was believed to have fled to Uganda; the matter is under investigation, so nothing in it is proven. De La Rue printed the 2,000-franc note introduced on 3 September 2024. John Rwangombwa was Governor from 2013 until February 2025, and came to the post from the government: he was Minister of Finance and Economic Planning from 2009, and earlier in the same ministry he directed the National Treasury Department, set up the Accountant General's Office in 2005 and became Rwanda's first Accountant General, then Permanent Secretary and Secretary to the Treasury; he had joined the Rwanda Revenue Authority in 1998 and was Deputy Commissioner of Customs for Operations by 2002.

PRESSURE IN PRACTICE

Government pressure reported in 0 of 37 quarters, 2010–2018.

2012-11 · public pressure. Rwanda's Senate summoned central bank governor Claver Gatete, who appeared with finance minister John Rwangombwa, over the Rwandan franc's slide against major currencies; the franc had weakened 1.3 percent against the dollar in the first half of the year. Gatete put the pressure down to speculation, saying investors who heard donors were cutting aid rushed to buy and stock foreign exchange, and said the central bank had put US$327.8 million into the foreign exchange market since 2011. The report gives the week, not the exact hearing date. Senate summons central bank governor over weakening franc (The New Times, Rwanda)
