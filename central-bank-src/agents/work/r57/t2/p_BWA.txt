===== BOX =====
BANK OF BOTSWANA
Botswana
The Bank of Botswana issues the pula, which it manages against the South African rand, moving the rate in small steps within a narrow band. One case is recorded and it ended in acquittal. A woman arrested on 17 October 2019 was accused, with others including former president Ian Khama and former intelligence head Isaac Kgosi, of stealing 100 billion pula from the bank. High Court judge Zein Kebonang discharged and acquitted her of unexplained property and a false passport declaration, the state withdrew a terrorism financing charge, and he found the accusations fabricated and referred the investigator to police for perjury prosecution. The accusations against the others remain unproven. One career move is documented: Linah Kelebogile Mohohlo joined the bank on 20 April 1976, worked at the International Monetary Fund from 1994 to 1996 under its Special Appointee Programme, in the African Department and the Monetary and Capital Markets Department, and later served as the bank's Governor and Chairman of the Board from 20 October 1999 until she retired in October 2016, having earlier been Deputy Governor and the founding director of its foreign department. Northern Trust works for the bank on measuring and explaining how its investments have performed and on training staff, which the source describes as reporting and training rather than management of its assets. The 10 pula polymer note that entered circulation on 1 February 2018 was designed and printed by De La Rue. EY audits the accounts. Botswana scores 23 of 100 on exposure to outside influence, where 100 means fully exposed.

Botswana's Directorate of Public Prosecutions, headed by Stephen Tiroyakgosi, called in Bank of Botswana governor Moses Pelaelo to hear his version of the P100 billion the state alleged had been looted from the central bank by former president Ian Khama and former intelligence chief Isaac Kgosi. Tiroyakgosi said Pelaelo told them there is no money missing from the Bank of Botswana, and that the investigation was continuing. The report gives no exact date for the meeting, only that it had happened recently. DPP Summons BoB Governor over Khama, Kgosi (The Botswana Gazette)
2019-05 · investigation of state accounts at the bank. Botswana's Directorate on Corruption and Economic Crime confirmed it was investigating unaccounted-for funds on government accounts held at the Bank of Botswana, after the Auditor General reported that P22 billion in receipts and payments remained unaccounted for. The Financial Intelligence Agency was reported to have opened its own investigation into the same accounts; its Director General would neither confirm nor deny it. No start date is given for either investigation. DCEC, FIA launch investigations (The Botswana Gazette)
