MONETARY AUTHORITY OF MACAO
Macao
Macao's money, the pataca, is issued by the Monetary Authority of Macao, and in December 2025 it was held at a fixed rate against the United States dollar. The top note of the Bank of China Macau series, the 1,000-pataca note, is printed by Hong Kong Note Printing Limited; Macao's other note-issuing bank is BNU. One case is documented, and it is an administrative finding by a foreign regulator rather than a criminal conviction: on 15 September 2005 the US Treasury named Banco Delta Asia, a bank licensed and supervised in Macao, a primary money laundering concern under Section 311 of the USA PATRIOT Act, and FinCEN proposed a rule cutting it off from US correspondent accounts. Treasury said the bank had served North Korean government agencies and front companies for more than 20 years, handled the bulk of North Korea's precious metal sales, and alleged that senior bank officials accepted large cash deposits, including counterfeit US currency, and agreed to put the notes into circulation; Under Secretary Stuart Levey said Macao needs significant improvement in its money laundering controls, and the bank denied wrongdoing. Its law leaves it little independence from government: 0.271 of 1 on one index, the lowest of 155 places, with its power to refuse lending to the government scored at zero. It has two borrowing lines with China's central bank, one signed on 5 December 2025 that runs both ways for 50 billion yuan, or 57 billion patacas, with no end date, and an earlier line of 30 billion renminbi from December 2019 whose standing is not known. It scores 32 of 100, where 100 means fully exposed to outside influence.

2005 · money laundering at a supervised bank; foreign regulator finding on weak local controls · official finding. On 15 September 2005 the US Treasury named Banco Delta Asia, a bank licensed and supervised in Macao, a primary money laundering concern under Section 311 of the USA PATRIOT Act, and FinCEN proposed a rule cutting it off from US correspondent accounts. Treasury said the bank had served North Korean government agencies and front companies for more than 20 years, handled the bulk of North Korea's precious metal sales, and helped agents make multi-million dollar cash deposits and withdrawals. It alleged that senior bank officials accepted large cash deposits, including counterfeit US currency, and agreed to put the notes into circulation, and that the bank serviced an account for a known international drug trafficker. Treasury said the bank tailored its services to North Korea with little oversight or control, and Under Secretary Stuart Levey said Macao needs significant improvement in its money laundering controls. These are the allegations of the US authorities; the bank denied wrongdoing, and the finding is an administrative one, not a criminal conviction. Treasury Designates Banco Delta Asia as Primary Money Laundering Concern under USA PATRIOT Act (US Treasury, 15 Sep 2005)

PRESSURE IN PRACTICE

2025-06-26 · statutory arrangement letting the head of the executive appoint the bank's board. Macao's Chief Executive, Sam Hou Fai, appointed Vong Sin Man, a worker of the Statistics and Census Service, as a member of the board of directors of the Monetary Authority of Macao, from 1 July to 31 December 2025. The order is made under article 50 of the Basic Law and articles 4(2)(a), 14 and 15(1) and (2) of the Monetary Authority's Statute, approved by Decree-Law 14/96/M of 11 March 1996, which the order shows is still the law in force: under that statute the head of the executive appoints the Authority's board. The pay for the post is borne by the Monetary Authority. This is a standing arrangement with no end date. Despacho do Chefe do Executivo n.o 120/2025 (Boletim Oficial da RAEM, Serie II, n.o 27)
