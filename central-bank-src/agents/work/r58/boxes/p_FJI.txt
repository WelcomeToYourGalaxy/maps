RESERVE BANK OF FIJI
Fiji
The Reserve Bank of Fiji, created in 1973, issues the Fijian dollar and, as of December 2025, holds it at a pre-announced rate measured against the United States and Australian dollars. Fiji scores 30 of 100 for exposure to outside influence, where 100 means fully exposed. Its weakest side is corruption and secrecy, 56 of 100, mainly because the country's laws hide who owns money: it scores 77.4 of 100 on a financial secrecy measure. The independence the law gives the bank is thin, 0.435 of 1 and rank 154 of 191, and it scores 5.5 of 15 for what it tells the public. One career move is recorded, which is not an accusation. Inia Naiyaga spent 40 years as a central banker, 25 of them on the bank's executive management team and five as deputy governor of the National Reserve Bank of Tonga, and retired as deputy governor in Fiji in May 2014. He had advised the board of SUN Insurance since 2016 and became an independent director in April 2018, and the board then appointed him managing director from 6 June, after chief executive Lolesh K. Sharma resigned. In 2006 the bank signed an agreement with De La Rue International Limited of the United Kingdom to print a new design series, including a 100-dollar note for the first time.

Inia Naiyaga (Deputy Governor, Reserve Bank of Fiji, retired May 2014; consultant to the SUN Insurance board from 2016; independent director April 2018; Managing Director from 6 June 2018): central bank deputy governor to Managing Director of SUN Insurance. The Fiji Sun reports that SUN Insurance's board, through chairman Padam Raj Lala, appointed Inia Naiyaga as Managing Director effective 6 June, after chief executive Lolesh K. Sharma resigned. The same article states Naiyaga had been a consultant to the SUN board since 2016 and became an independent director in April 2018. On his public career it says he retired as Deputy Governor of the Reserve Bank of Fiji in May 2014, sat on the central bank's executive management team for 25 years, spent 40 years as a central banker, and served five years as Deputy Governor of the National Reserve Bank of Tonga. SUN Insurance is an insurance company, which this project counts as private finance; the article itself describes SUN only as a '100 per cent Fiji owned entity' and does not use the words private or state-owned. PRESSURE IN PRACTICE

Not covered by Binder's 2010–2018 survey.

2009-04-15 · dismissal. Central Banking reported that government officials removed Reserve Bank of Fiji governor Savenaca Narube and replaced him with deputy governor Sada Reddy, hours before the Fiji dollar was devalued by 20%. The report says the new government had come to power the previous week and was led by the army. No official reason for the removal is given; the report notes Narube had recently warned about the economy and forecast a 0.3% contraction for 2009.
