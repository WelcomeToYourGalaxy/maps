CENTRAL BANK OF SOLOMON ISLANDS
Solomon Islands
The Solomon Islands dollar is issued by the Central Bank of Solomon Islands, created in 1976 and holding the currency at a crawling peg to the US dollar as of December 2025, meaning the rate is moved in small steps announced in advance. The country scores 31 of 100 for exposure to outside influence, where 100 means fully exposed, and the weakest side is corruption and secrecy around the bank. The bank is near the bottom of 112 places for what it tells the public, at 3 of 15. One case is recorded: a former employee, Bobongi, was convicted on 39 counts of money laundering and two counts of larceny and embezzlement for stealing more than 1.7 million dollars in old banknotes in 2007 to 2009, while the bank was destroying them, and was sentenced to nine years. Note Printing Australia's customer list says it has produced and supplied polymer banknotes to the bank, with no date or value given. Crown Agents Investment Management manages about 95 percent of the bank's allocation of special drawing rights, a reserve asset issued by the International Monetary Fund, and the Auditor General's Office audits the accounts.

PRESSURE IN PRACTICE

Not covered by Binder's 2010–2018 survey.

2012 · statutory arrangement letting the government direct the bank. Under the Central Bank of Solomon Islands Act 2012, which the bank publishes, section 37 says the Minister for Finance may, with the approval of Cabinet, give the Central Bank written directions where the Minister is of the opinion that coordination of monetary and fiscal policies or the public interest requires it, and must publish those directions in the Gazette and lay them before Parliament. Section 41(3) says the other directors of the Board are appointed by the Minister after consultation with the Governor, and section 41(1) says the Governor is appointed by the Governor-General on Cabinet's recommendation. Under section 44 the Governor, Deputy Governor and other Board members may be removed before their term ends only on grounds of permanent incapacity or serious misconduct, and the Governor only on a tribunal's recommendation. The Central Bank of Solomon Islands (Amendment) Act 2023 does not amend sections 37, 41 or 44. This is a standing arrangement with no end date. Central Bank of Solomon Islands Act 2012 (Central Bank of Solomon Islands)
