NATIONAL BANK OF TAJIKISTAN
Tajikistan
The National Bank of Tajikistan issues the somoni, which it keeps within a band of 2 per cent or less against the US dollar. One case is recorded, as an official finding: in March 2008 the International Monetary Fund's Executive Board found the bank had supplied inaccurate information on the cotton sector, international reserves, net domestic assets and credit policy, reporting reserves of about 450 million US dollars when they were roughly a third of that and concealing that reserves were pledged as collateral for cotton loans; Tajikistan was required to repay 47.4 million US dollars. No government pressure was reported from 2010 to 2018. It has one swap line, an arrangement to borrow another central bank's currency: 3 billion yuan with China, signed on 3 September 2015 to run three years and extendable by agreement, drawn on for 1 million yuan in January 2016; whether it is in force is not known. Its commemorative 100-somoni note, circulated on 30 October 2025, was printed by Giesecke+Devrient of Germany, that note alone and not the regular series. Its books are checked from outside by RSM Tajikistan LLC of Dushanbe, whose managing partner signed the independent auditor's report on the 2023 financial statements. Worth noticing is that the firm holds a licence to audit banks issued by the National Bank itself, so the body being audited also licenses its auditor. Earlier audits were done by Deloitte and by Ernst & Young Ford Rhodes Sidat Hyder.

PRESSURE IN PRACTICE

Government pressure reported in 0 of 37 quarters, 2010–2018.

2011-06-28 · statutory arrangement letting the government direct the bank. The Law of the Republic of Tajikistan “On the National Bank of Tajikistan”, No. 722 of 28 June 2011, signed by President Emomali Rahmon, puts every seat on the bank's Board in the President's hands. Article 74(4) says the Chairman of the National Bank and his deputies are appointed to office and released from office by decree of the President, with the decrees submitted to the lower house of parliament for approval, and article 74(3) says the Board's three independent members are approved by the President on the Chairman's proposal. Article 74(6) says the President sets the salary of the Chairman and his deputies.
