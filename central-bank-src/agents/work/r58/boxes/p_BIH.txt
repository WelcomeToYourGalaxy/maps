CENTRAL BANK OF BOSNIA AND HERZEGOVINA
Bosnia
The convertible mark is issued by the Central Bank of Bosnia and Herzegovina under a currency board: marks are put into circulation only against convertible foreign currency the bank holds, at 1.95583 marks to the euro, and its monetary liabilities may never exceed its foreign currency assets. It is one of the 63 central banks that own the Bank for International Settlements, and it has joined the World Bank Treasury's Reserve Advisory and Management Partnership, which offers advice, training and asset management, with the announcement stating the membership rather than which of those services the bank takes. Its law scores 0.899 of 1 for independence, 11th of 155 countries, and no government pressure was reported in the quarters from 2010 to 2018. One case is documented: in 2023 a criminal complaint accused governor Senad Softic of suspected wrongdoing in several matters, including a project to acquire an office building in Pale and a procedure to buy land in Sarajevo. The prosecutor's office said in February 2023 that it had opened a case and was verifying the allegations, which remain unproven, while the bank said examining allegations is not an investigation and that reports of one were untrue. Grant Thornton d.o.o. Banja Luka audited the 2016 accounts. The state has twice acted on the bank. On 25 June 2019 two of the five members of its Governing Board were dismissed by the country's tripartite Presidency, with the reasons unclear. And in April 2025 the Council of Ministers adopted a revised state budget that settled an arbitration liability out of the bank's profits; the bank suspended the transfer of 244.3 million marks, part of its 412.6 million marks of 2024 net profit, to the Ministry of Finance and Treasury, saying the move endangered its assets. The bank holds 3.5 tonnes of gold and scores 39 of 100, where 100 means fully exposed to outside influence. One career move is recorded. The Central Bank of Bosnia and Herzegovina's own profile page gives Jasmina Selimovic's dated career: at the Faculty of Economics in Sarajevo since 2004, its Dean from 2018 and renewed in 2022; in the Fiscal Council's advisory group from 2011 to 2015; an independent member of the Supervisory Board of Raiffeisen Bank Bosnia and Herzegovina in 2018; president of the board of the Sarajevo Canton Institute of Health Insurance since 2021; and a member of the bank's Governing Board from 3 January 2024. The central bank's page leaves the ownership of Raiffeisen Bank Bosnia and Herzegovina unstated; that bank's own ownership page lists Raiffeisen SEE Region Holding GmbH as sole owner at 100 per cent, with no state shareholder, which is what makes this a move involving a private bank. Moving between jobs is not wrongdoing.

1 documented career move between the bank and private finance, the IMF or the World Bank:

Jasmina Selimovic (member of the CBBH Governing Board from 3 January 2024; Governor and chair of the Governing Board): from independent member of the Supervisory Board of Raiffeisen Bank BiH (2018) to member of the CBBH Governing Board (January 2024) and Governor ; from president of the Board of Directors of the Sarajevo Canton Institute of Health Insurance (since 2021) to the CBBH Governing Board. The central bank's own profile page gives her career in order: employed at the Faculty of Economics in Sarajevo since 2004, where she is a full professor and was elected Dean in 2018 with a second four-year mandate in 2022; member of the Advisory Group of the Fiscal Council of Bosnia and Herzegovina 2011-2015, representing the Prime Minister of the Federation of BiH; independent member of the Supervisory Board of Raiffeisen Bank BiH in 2018; president of the Board of Directors of the Sarajevo Canton Institute of Health Insurance since 2021; and appointed by the Presidency of Bosnia and Herzegovina to the CBBH Governing Board on 3 January 2024 for a six-year mandate. The CBBH page does not say who owns Raiffeisen Bank BiH. The bank's own ownership page (https://www.raiffeisenbank.ba/en/about-us/about-raiffeisen/who-we-are/raiffeisen-bosna-i-hercegovina.html) lists a single owner, Raiffeisen SEE Region Holding GmbH at 100.00%, with no state shareholder, so the employer is privately owned; the page itself does not use the words private or commercial. The Sarajevo Canton Institute of Health Insurance is a statutory public body, and the Fiscal Council role was a government appointment. Jasmina Selimovic, Governor and Chairman of the Governing Board (Central Bank of Bosnia and Herzegovina)

PRESSURE IN PRACTICE

Government pressure reported in 0 of 37 quarters, 2010–2018.

2025-04-23 · government earmarks the bank's profit to pay a state debt. The Council of Ministers of Bosnia and Herzegovina adopted a revised 2025 draft budget for state institutions that provided for the liabilities arising from the Viaduct v BiH international arbitration to be settled out of the Central Bank of Bosnia and Herzegovina's profits; the bank, whose Governing Board had allocated 244.3 million convertible marks of its 412.6 million marks of 2024 net profit for transfer to the Ministry of Finance and Treasury, publicly suspended that transfer, saying the use of the money for arbitration debt would endanger its assets and the country's financial stability. Bosnia's Central Bank refuses budget transfer amid arbitration debt concerns - N1
2019-06-25 · board members sacked by the head of state. Two of the five members of the Governing Board of the Central Bank of Bosnia and Herzegovina were sacked by the country's tripartite Presidency, the collective head of state, in a move reported on 25 June 2019 whose stated reasons were unclear; both the Presidency and the central bank are governed by the ethnic power-sharing arrangements written into the Dayton peace settlement. Bosnian central bank board members sacked by president - Central Banking
