CENTRAL RESERVE BANK OF EL SALVADOR
El Salvador
El Salvador's central bank is the Central Reserve Bank of El Salvador, and the currency is listed as both the colon and the US dollar. The IMF's 2023 classification puts the country among places with no separate legal tender of their own, using the US dollar as their anchor; legal changes made private acceptance of Bitcoin voluntary and tax payments payable only in US dollars, listed as steps taken before an IMF loan approved on 26 February 2025. The bank's accounts are audited from outside by the Corte de Cuentas de la Republica, under a law still in force. Written independence from government scores 0.789 of 1, and no government pressure was reported in any of the 37 quarters from 2010 to 2018. No case at the bank is documented, which does not prove none happened. The US dollar is used in 16 places and makes up 89.08 per cent of world currency trading. El Salvador scores 37 of 100, where 100 means fully exposed to outside influence.

PRESSURE IN PRACTICE

Diario El Mundo reported that Nicolás Martínez, president of the Banco Central de Reserva, answered a summons from a Legislative Assembly commission investigating the debt the government had taken on and how it was used during the COVID-19 pandemic, and resigned the day after appearing. OWNERSHIP AND OUTSIDE CONTROL

Official dollarization (US dollar as sole legal tender) (dollarization)

The IMF AREAER 2023 classification (end-April 2023) lists Ecuador, El Salvador, Marshall Islands, Micronesia, Palau, Panama and Timor-Leste as having no separate legal tender with the US dollar as anchor.
Ecuador adopted the US dollar in January 2000 at 25,000 sucres per dollar under the Ley para la Transformación Económica (Registro Oficial Suplemento 34, 13 March 2000); Article 94 of the Código Orgánico Monetario y Financiero (2014) makes the dollar the currency of transactions.
In El Salvador, legal reforms made private-sector acceptance of Bitcoin voluntary and tax payments payable only in US dollars, listed as prior actions for the IMF EFF approved on 26 February 2025.
