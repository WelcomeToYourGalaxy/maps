CENTRAL BANK OF CHILE
Chile
Chile's central bank is the Central Bank of Chile, which issues the Chilean peso. It scores 24 of 100, where 100 means fully exposed to outside influence, and no government pressure was reported between 2010 and 2018. The Constitutional Convention elected to write a replacement for the 1980 constitution drafted new articles on the bank, and on 5 April 2022 its president Rosanna Costa wrote to the Convention on the Council's behalf setting out what it objected to. The main objection was the removal provision: one third of the members of Congress could have asked the Supreme Court to remove councillors, which the bank said exposed it to political judgements that would diminish its autonomy and its working. It asked that the power to request removal be limited to a majority of the councillors or to the President of the Republic, and that the organic law's standard be written in, so that the conduct must have been the main and direct cause of significant damage to the country's economy. It also asked that productive diversification be struck from the list of its objectives, as not a monetary task. Voters rejected the draft on 4 September 2022. Two cases are recorded, both still under investigation. On 24 July 2012 three staff allegedly tried to take about 38 million pesos in banknotes being processed for destruction, and a fourth was detained the next day; the bank told the police economic crimes unit and began legal action. In March 2022 someone posing as the finance chief of the US firm RVK sent a false invoice for 205,000 dollars from an address one letter off the real one, and the bank paid. It complained to prosecutors in May 2022, said no official appeared to have taken part deliberately, and dismissed its accounting management head that June; he sued in December, seeking 267 million pesos. Part of the state's sovereign wealth funds is run by outside firms the bank picks and supervises, BlackRock holding the high yield and corporate bond portfolios and Nomura Asset Management part of the high yield bonds. KPMG audits the accounts.

PRESSURE IN PRACTICE

Government pressure reported in 0 of 37 quarters, 2010–2018.

2022-04-05 · attempted law change. Chile's Constitutional Convention, elected to write a replacement for the 1980 constitution, drafted new articles on the Banco Central de Chile in its Commission 6 on justice systems, autonomous oversight bodies and constitutional reform. On 5 April 2022 the bank's president Rosanna Costa wrote to the president of the Convention on behalf of the bank's Council, in official letter 114, setting out what the Council objected to. Its main objection was to the removal provision: the draft allowed one third of the members of Congress to ask the Supreme Court to remove councillors, which the bank said exposed the institution to a complex situation and to political judgements that would diminish its autonomy and its working. The Council asked that the power to request removal be limited to a majority of the councillors or to the President of the Republic, and that the existing organic law's standard be written in, so that a removed councillor's conduct must have been the main and direct cause of significant damage to the country's economy. It also asked that productive diversification be struck from the list of the bank's objectives, on the ground that it is not a monetary task, and that the rule in the current constitution's article 109 barring the bank from discriminating between parties be restored, as the draft had dropped it. On the governance articles the letter said changes should not diminish the effectiveness of the institution's autonomy, and it noted that appointment by the President of the Republic had not harmed the bank's autonomy. When the plenary voted on 4 May 2022 it approved the bank's existence and its autonomy but sent the articles on its powers, limits and the appointment of councillors back to committee for lack of the 103 votes needed. Voters rejected the draft constitution on 4 September 2022, so none of it took effect. Oficio ORD. No 114 del Banco Central de Chile a la Presidenta de la Convencion Constitucional, 5 de abril de 2022
