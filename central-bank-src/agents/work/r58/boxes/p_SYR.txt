CENTRAL BANK OF SYRIA
Syria
The Central Bank of Syria issues the Syrian pound, which moves within a band of about 5 percent against the US dollar. Government pressure was reported in 11 of 37 quarters between 2010 and 2018, each time pressing the bank to ease and demanding that it print money. One case runs from 2005 to 2011: a Vienna court found that the Austrian banknote printer OeBS had overcharged the bank by 14 percent on banknote contracts and that the kickback returned to local decision makers. Prosecutors had told the court that former OeBS employees paid bribes to officials at Syrian and Azerbaijani banks in exchange for contracts to print money, an allegation as reported. OeBS managers were convicted in 2014 and again on retrial in 2018, and no Syrian official was named or tried in those proceedings. Syria appoints no private audit firm: article 68(3) of its Basic Monetary Law of 2002 gives the auditing of the bank's records and accounts to the Central Organization for Financial Control, the state's own supreme audit institution, and the bank's published reports carry no independent auditor's report. A circulating 500-pound note, part of the series that dropped two zeros from the pound, is printed by Goznak, the Russian state currency works. Syria scores 38 of 100, where 100 means fully exposed to outside influence, and 15 of 100 on the Corruption Perceptions Index.

PRESSURE IN PRACTICE

Government pressure reported in 11 of 37 quarters, 2010–2018. Pressing to cut rates or ease: 11; to tighten: 0. Threats to replace leaders: 0; to change the law: 0; demands to print money: 11; in election periods: 0.

2002-03-17 · statutory arrangement letting the government direct the bank. Syria's Basic Monetary Law No. 23 of 2002, issued in Damascus on 17 March 2002 by President Bashar al-Assad, places the bank and its policy-making council under the government. Article 1 says the Monetary and Credit Council works within the general economic directions of the state approved by the Council of Ministers, and sets monetary policy in line with the state's general strategy. Article 8(3) says the Council's decisions are communicated to the Minister of Economy and Foreign Trade, who may suspend for up to seven days any decision he considers contrary to the interest of the state, and if the disagreement continues he must refer the matter to the Council of Ministers to decide. Article 69 says the Governor is appointed, and his service terminated, by decree; article 2 puts the Council's non-bank seats, among them assistant ministers of economy, finance, agriculture and industry and the head of the State Planning Authority, in the gift of a decree on their ministries' proposal; and article 71 says the Minister of Economy and Foreign Trade names the four supervising directors who sit with the Governor on the management committee. قانون النقد الأساسي — القانون رقم 23 لعام 2002 (Central Bank of Syria)
