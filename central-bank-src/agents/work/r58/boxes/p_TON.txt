NATIONAL RESERVE BANK OF TONGA
Tonga
The National Reserve Bank of Tonga issues the pa'anga (TOP), which follows a crawling peg or a band of 1% or less against the US dollar. It scores 5.5 of 15 on what it tells the public. On the map's scale, 0 means shielded from outside influence and 100 means fully exposed; Tonga scores 31. Two cases are documented. In 2024-2025 Tonga's Anti-Corruption Commissioner sued the bank and its directors, alleging that a 2024 staff payment (for example $6,600 to each of 73 staff) was an unlawful bonus linked to record profit; the board had described it as a one-off anniversary payout, and on 20 November 2025 the Court of Appeal dismissed the proceeding. In 2017-2019 a Senior Exchange Control Officer forged a Government bond to support another person's New Zealand visa application and asked for $2,500 pa'anga. She pleaded guilty and her employment was terminated; the Supreme Court of Tonga ruled on 21 May 2019. The bank's accounts for the year to 30 June 1993, published in the Tonga Government Gazette, carry an auditor's report signed by Coopers & Lybrand at Suva, Fiji, on 21 September 1993. De La Rue printed the 100-pa'anga note in the family introduced on 4 December 2023.

2 documented cases at the central bank:

2024-2025 · conflict of interest · case dismissed. Tonga's Anti-Corruption Commissioner sued the bank and its directors, alleging that a 2024 staff payment (for example $6,600 to each of 73 staff) was an unlawful bonus linked to record profit, a breach of trust and misfeasance in public office. The board had described it as a one-off anniversary payout. On 20 November 2025 the Court of Appeal dismissed the proceedings, finding no reasonable cause of action against the bank and that the claim against the directors also failed. National Reserve Bank of Tonga v Anti-Corruption Commissioner [2025] TOCA 25 (Court of Appeal of Tonga, PacLII)
2017-2019 · theft or fraud · convicted. A Senior Exchange Control Officer at the National Reserve Bank of Tonga forged a Government bond to support another person's New Zealand visa application and asked for $2,500 pa'anga. She pleaded guilty to forgery, knowingly dealing with forged documents and obtaining money by false pretenses, and her employment was terminated. On May 21, 2019 the Supreme Court of Tonga gave her a two-year sentence, fully suspended, and ordered $800 compensation. Former Reserve Bank officer gets suspended sentence for serious fraud (Matangi Tonga)

PRESSURE IN PRACTICE

Not covered by Binder's 2010–2018 survey.

1988 · statutory arrangement letting the government direct the bank. The National Reserve Bank of Tonga Act, Chapter 16.12, 2020 Revised Edition, puts the bank's board in the government's hands and gives Cabinet the last word on policy disputes. Section 10(1) says the Minister, with the consent of Cabinet, appoints five directors and appoints the Chairman and Deputy Chairman; section 11(1) says the Governor is appointed by Cabinet on the Minister's recommendation; and section 11(3) says the Governor may be removed from office by Cabinet, on the Minister's recommendation made at the Board's request, for permanent incapacity, serious misconduct or failure to perform his duties. Section 50(1) says the Minister may, with the consent of Cabinet, issue the bank written directives, and section 50(4) says that where the Minister and the Board disagree about a policy the Board has determined, the Minister must submit the disagreement to Cabinet to determine the policy to be adopted by the bank. This is a standing arrangement with no end date. National Reserve Bank of Tonga Act, CAP. 16.12, 2020 Revised Edition (Attorney General's Office, Tonga)
