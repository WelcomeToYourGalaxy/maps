Basemap sound — pick one for each

Every clip is about forty seconds and loops in the map. Play them, note the ones you want, and tell me the names. Anything here can be hosted on your own site: radio aporee field recordings in the public domain or under Creative Commons Attribution, spacecraft and seismometer measurements from NASA and EarthScope, which are not under copyright.

Nothing here comes from a video-sharing site. Volume is matched across every clip, so a quiet one is quiet on purpose.
