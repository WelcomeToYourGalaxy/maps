Records with no date are always shown. Dates are the source's: when a clash happened, when a base opened and closed, when a unit was formed and disbanded, when a case was registered or decided.

How dots are drawn

Filled dot: in use now, or the source records no end Ring: closed, disbanded or over, as the source records it Larger dot: more people killed (armed clashes), or more spending (country shading is darker)
