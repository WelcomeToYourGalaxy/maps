Why some famous programs aren’t on this map
This map is only about individual, household, village or neighbourhood self-sufficiency. Programs aimed at a whole country’s independence are a different subject and are excluded:

National commercial food output: India’s Green Revolution, China’s grain self-sufficiency, Indonesia’s rice Swasembada, Saudi desert-wheat, Italy’s Battle for Grain, Russia’s food-security doctrine, Japan’s food-ratio targets, the EU Common Agricultural Policy.

National industrial autarky / import substitution: Nazi Germany’s Four Year Plan, Fascist Italy’s Autarchia, Soviet Five-Year Plans, Latin-American ISI, India’s Licence Raj and Atmanirbhar Bharat, Turkish étatism, the Burmese Way to Socialism.

National energy independence: the US Project Independence, Brazil’s Proálcool ethanol, France’s Messmer nuclear plan, Japan’s Sunshine Project, Denmark’s energy drive.

National fiscal / monetary independence: Romania’s debt-free drive, the Buy American Act, ‘Buy British’, Albania’s foreign-debt ban, Israel’s Tzena austerity.

National strategic materials & state doctrines: the US WWII synthetic-rubber program, German synthetic oil & Buna rubber, Japan’s strategic stockpiles, North Korea’s Juche, Mao’s national Self-Reliance doctrine, Iran’s Resistance Economy, Albania’s total autarky.

What this map counts — and what it leaves out: it plots representative landmark examples of each kind of self-sufficiency institution — one or a few markers per type, not every occurrence. The countless individual, local and sub-national instances (every single savings club, garden plot, co-op, common or local programme) are not mapped one by one; the aim is to chart the world’s types of citizen self-sufficiency, not to be a complete census of them. Those uncounted instances are, almost without exception, individual local examples of the very types already shown here — one village’s savings club, one town’s community garden, one valley’s irrigation commons — not different kinds of institution. Where a local practice is genuinely a new kind, it is added here as its own marker rather than left in this excluded tail.
