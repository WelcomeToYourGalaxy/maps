# The twelve-card `displaced` sweep

Every live card whose `displaced_label` mentions demolition. Each figure was read back
out of the outcome document it comes from, and the verb the document attaches to it was
recorded. Classes: **(a)** a count of households or people whose homes the document
records as demolished, no relocation verb attached; **(b)** a count with a relocation
verb attached; **(c)** a count of people "affected" with nothing more, which under the
standing rule may not sit in `displaced`.

No card fell into class (c). No plan-reprint check fired.

## Figures that may need to come off the field

None under the standing rule. Two were checked because their labels read as "affected"
and both turned out to be narrower than that:

- **zhejiang-urban-environment-resettlement** (4,628). The label read "people affected
  by house demolition". The ICR splits its 7,025 project-affected people into 2,397
  affected by land use and 4,628 affected by house demolition, and the annex table
  counts shop and enterprise demolition in a separate column. The figure is the
  house-demolition part of the total, not the total, so it is a class (a) demolition
  count and not a bare count of affected people. The ICR adds "All project affected
  persons (PAPs) were resettled according to the RAPs", a statement about the 7,025.
- **sichuan-urban-development-four-cities** (3,262). The label read "affected by the
  resettlement through land acquisition, house demolition or both". The ICR uses a
  relocation verb for the same figure in its outcomes section, so it is class (b). But
  the annex's own wording means the 3,262 includes households that lost land and not
  houses. Table 4.1 counts house demolition separately at 1,903 rural and 716 urban
  households, and enterprise and shop relocation separately again at 289 units. A
  footnote says the Mianyang and Suining house-demolition people are already inside the
  land-acquisition figures, so the two cannot be added. If the lead prefers a pure
  demolition count here, 2,619 households is what the demolition rows give. That would
  change the numeric `displaced` field, which `merge_textfix.py` cannot write.

## Two least certain calls

1. **han-river-urban-environment-resettlement** (83), called (a). The ICR's own borrower
   section says "Necessary lands were acquired, demolishing and resettlements were
   completed in accordance with the RAPs, and all affected households were relocated",
   and the safeguards section records that in Shayang six households took cash
   compensation and "72 families opted for resettling in newly constructed" apartments -
   78 households, where the 83 demolished are "mostly from the Shayang landfill
   subproject". A relocation verb is in the document and close to the figure, but not
   attached to the 83, so the card is written as (a). A reading of (b) is defensible.
2. **sichuan-urban-development-four-cities** (3,262), called (b). One figure, two verbs,
   in one document. The card is written as (b) because "the 3,262 households that were
   relocated" is the document's own sentence, but the annex sentence shows the figure is
   wider than a count of homes lost. This is the one card of the twelve where the figure
   itself, not its label, is open to question.
