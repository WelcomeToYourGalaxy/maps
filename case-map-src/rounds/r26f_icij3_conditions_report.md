## Cases where terms could not be established

None. Five projects have no disclosed completion report (P114827, P090585, P118783, P094488, P120960);
of the three of those in this set, Gujarat (P114827), Punjab/Dehlon (P090585) and Donsin (P120960),
the appraisal documents are disclosed and carry both the safeguards table and the legal covenants
table, so the terms are established from the PAD rather than from a data sheet.

Two near-misses worth recording:

- Bahia (P081436): the completion report has no safeguards table and names no policy numbers. The
  four triggered policies were taken from the IEG review, which names them by title rather than by OP
  number, so the paragraph names them by title too and gives no OP numbers.
- Philippines (P079935): the completion report, in the IEG reviewer's words, failed to document which
  policies were triggered; the list comes from the IEG review, which prints the indigenous peoples
  policy once as "OP/BP 1.10" and later as OP 4.10. The paragraph names the policies in words.

## Weakest two paragraphs

1. **salem-tnudp-squatter-resettlement.** This is the only case where the safeguard policies were not
   fixed at appraisal: the appraisal document says applicability "will be determined at a subproject
   level" through the Environmental and Social Framework's screening tools. The paragraph therefore
   describes a process rather than a decision, and the three policies it names are marked in the
   appraisal table for the project as a whole, not for the Salem or Chennai works in particular. Nothing
   opened names the plan, date or terms for the Salem sub-project where the 65 families were moved, so
   the paragraph says what the framework required of any such sub-project and no more.

2. **drc-selembao-loange-compensation.** The terms here are split across two sites and two documents.
   The resettlement and compensation plan that was read is the Loange bridge plan, so the sequencing
   rule (plan carried out before works) and the implementation arrangements are Loange's. For the
   Drève de Selembao the only material opened is the completion report's statement that the site was
   occupied and that a plan worth US$0.533 million was drawn up for 233 people; no plan document for
   that site was found, so the paragraph states no Selembao-specific requirement. The programmatic
   screening process described at the start is project-wide and does not say which policies ended up
   applying to either site.
