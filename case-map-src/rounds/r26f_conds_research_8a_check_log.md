# Independent check: r26f_conds_research_8a

86 records, 16 with a non-empty `conditions_add`. All 16 were checked against the cited
document opened directly (World Bank documents search API → `documents1.worldbank.org`
PDF; `adb.org` document paths; the Early Warning System mirror for the ADB Jawa-1 report;
the EIB project page). None was blanked: every entry had a real document behind it. Ten
needed corrections, six stand as written.

## Weakest entries let stand

1. **cairo-metro-3-zamalek** — the only entry resting on a lender web page rather than a
   financing document. The language ("the project has to meet the environmental and social
   requirements of the Bank") is the EIB's own statement about this operation, so it clears
   failure #1, but the finance contract is unpublished and the page's resettlement wording
   is about "acceptable progress", not a disbursement condition.
2. **jawa-1-lng-power-fishers** — after stripping the four descriptive items, what remains
   is reporting obligations and the sponsors' commitments. Nothing ties disbursement to
   them, and the loan agreement is not published.
3. **samut-prakarn-klong-dan** — the compensation obligation is reported second-hand, in a
   Board committee's text, four to seven years after the loans, and it rests partly on Thai
   law. The loan agreements for 1410 and 1646-THA remain unpublished.
4. **plantar-minas-gerais** — a Project Information Document, not the purchase agreement,
   which is still unpublished. The payment-on-verification and FSC due-diligence statements
   are stated as requirements, but the document is a disclosure summary.
5. **transmigration-indonesia** — now carries only `admin`, and none of the covenants touch
   the harm on the card. It is the Third project's covenants standing for a programme the
   card runs from 1976 to 1994; the entry names the project, which is what keeps it honest.
6. **bhabadah-waterlogging-kjdrp** — the covenants are quoted from Schedule 6 of the RRP by
   the evaluation report, not from the loan agreement, which is not published.
