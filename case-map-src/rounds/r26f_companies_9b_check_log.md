# Check log - r26f_companies_9b

Independent check of 49 company descriptions. Every entry below was verified against a source opened in this session; sources the writer cited but that I did not open were removed from the entry. No entry ended fully blank: for the thin ones I was able to find a qualifying source (news report, filing or official page) rather than having to blank them, and the unsupported parts were cut instead.

## Changed entries

- **Philex Mining Corporation** - dropped "silver", the ticker "PX" and "2,590 employees" (all from a market-data aggregator only); added the PSE listing date. The company's own corporate profile gives incorporation in 1955, listing on the PSE since 23 November 1956, and copper-gold exploration; its own 2023 Integrated ACGR gives the principal office in Mandaluyong City.
- **Syrah Resources** - dropped "founded in 2007", "vanadium", the ticker and "374 employees". Syrah's 2025 annual report (lodged on ASX) gives natural graphite only, Balama in Cabo Delgado, the Vidalia anode plant in Louisiana, FY2025 revenue of US$33.4 million and 67kt of 2025 graphite output.
- **Petra Diamonds** - "three underground mines in South Africa" corrected: the FY2024 results announcement lists Cullinan and Finsch operating with Koffiefontein on care and maintenance ahead of a possible sale, plus Williamson in Tanzania.
- **Tozzi Green** - rewritten on a source the writer had not found. New Lines Magazine's Madagascar report states the firm is Italian, began as a family business in a town near Ravenna after the Second World War, is based in northern Italy, works mainly in hydro and rural electrification plus agribusiness, and had revenue of nearly US$103 million in 2023 with operations in Italy, Peru and Madagascar. The writer's "part of the Tozzi Holding group, within its Tozzi Renewable Energy branch" is supported by nothing opened and was removed.
- **Tiomin Resources** - dropped "Canadian" (no source opened states incorporation or head office); the Canadian Mining Journal and the March 2010 company release give TSX: TIO, Kwale and the Pukaqaqa half-interest, and by 2010 payment/royalty agreements over both.
- **Wildlife Works** - dropped "sells those credits to companies and investors"; the company's own page describes developing REDD+ projects that generate credits, not the sale channel.
- **Veolia Environnement** - "French company" was not on the press release. Veolia's Form 20-F on SEC EDGAR gives incorporation in France and a registered office in Paris, so the nationality now rests on that filing; revenue and headcount stand on the press release.
- **Saipem** - "wind" removed from the business lines; the "at a glance" PDF lists asset-based services, offshore drilling and energy carriers. Nationality and the Milan registered office now rest on Saipem's own shareholder Q&A document, which also places its shares on Borsa Italiana.
- **PowerChina** - removed revenue of 645.6bn yuan in 2025 and 161,478 employees: those came from a market-data aggregator page for the listed arm (Shanghai 601669) and no filing for them could be opened. Replaced with the properly dated figures from the government-sourced profile (US$42.4bn revenue in 2014; about 200,000 employees and 1,565 overseas projects in 110 countries at end-June 2015) and with "wholly state-owned", which China Daily's directory of central SOEs states.
- **Union Development Group (UDG)** - "property developer" removed. Treasury calls Dara Sakor ostensibly a tourism development and describes UDG as a PRC state-owned entity acting for or on behalf of a PRC official, but does not state UDG's line of business.
- **PT PLN** - "generates, transmits, distributes" trimmed to what Kontan reports (sells electricity nationwide; results presented at the Ministry of State-Owned Enterprises); added the 306.22 TWh sold in 2024 alongside the revenue and profit figures, which check out.
- **RATCH** - "the state Electricity Generating Authority of Thailand" trimmed to the name as the company's own page gives it; the 45 percent stake, 7 March 2000 founding, name change and 9,484 MW at 1 April 2026 all check out.
- **WTK Holdings** - "Malaysian group whose main business is logging" corrected: The Star's FY2024 segment figures put plantation (RM295.3m) above timber (RM203.9m). Added the subsidiary named for the Bintulu plywood closure, Cairnfield Sdn Bhd.
- **Shin Yang Group** - "which listed on Bursa Malaysia in 2010" removed. The Star (May 2010) reports only that Shin Yang Shipping was preparing an IPO for mid-2010; the completion of the listing is not in the source.
- **Proman** - "privately held" removed: the company's own page speaks of the "spirit" of a family-owned business but states no ownership. Capacity restated as the page gives it, "over 10 million tonnes a year of bulk chemicals".
- **WWF** - tightened to the financial statements' own wording (separate organisations not consolidated for lack of control; Delaware nonprofit corporation; US$486,056,494 operating revenue to 30 June 2024).
- **Societe Miniere de Dinguiraye (SMD)** - the tax and FODEL figures came from a paid advertorial on a Guinean news site and were dropped. Replaced with Mining Weekly's 2018 report on the renewal of the Lefa mining convention: SMD is a Nordgold subsidiary owning and operating Lefa, which supplied over 20 percent of Nordgold's group production, and the Republic of Guinea was to receive 15 percent of SMD's share capital in two tranches.
- **PT Permata Hijau Pasaman I** - no source names an entity with the "I". A 2024 ISPO notice names estates "Kebun Permata Hijau Pasaman unit 1" and "unit 2" of PT Permata Hijau Pasaman, which is the closest documented match, so the entry now describes that company and its unit 1 (1,247 ha, 726 ha planted, 9,712 t FFB in the year to June 2024) and claims no separate legal entity. "Singapore-listed" was removed from the Wilmar reference - the RSPO filings do not state it.
- **PT Mikgro Metal Perdana** - "August 2016" changed to "in 2016": Petromindo's 19 September 2016 report says the Supreme Court cancelled the permit "last month" and gives no date.
- **Shanghai Kangshuo Co Ltd** - added that it had relocated, which the China Daily report states.
- **Roads Department of Georgia** - dropped the Tbilisi street address and "Ministry of Regional Development and Infrastructure", both of which came from a third-party organisation directory. The Law of Georgia on Roads (Articles 9, 17, 22-1) supplies the functions and names the Ministry of Infrastructure as the body setting state road policy and hearing appeals.
- **PT Intraco Penta** - dropped "founded in 1970" (the two third-party profiles found disagree; one gives 10 May 1975) and the 2025 revenue, loss and 496-employee figures from a market-data aggregator. Replaced with Bisnis.com's June 2025 report: heavy and construction equipment, fabrication, infrastructure and power generation; 2024 operating revenue Rp954.68bn, 93.61 percent from heavy and construction equipment, net loss Rp117.84bn.
- **Sarawak Oil Palms** - dropped "7,513 employees" (aggregator only; the 2025 annual report would not load and the 2023 report's headcount sits in an unreadable part of the file). The 2023 annual report confirms 1968, the Miri registered office, Bursa main board SOP (5126) and the plantation/milling/refining/trading/property segments.
- **Yakjin** - the recruitment-site profile the writer used is not a qualifying source, so "founded in 1978" and the count of eight factories (five Vietnam, two Cambodia, one Indonesia) were dropped. The Phnom Penh Post and the Worker Rights Consortium factory record support: South Korean garment maker headquartered in Seoul, clients including Gap, Walmart, Pink and American Eagle Outfitters, factories in Cambodia, and Carlyle Group taking a majority stake (reported as 70 percent) in December 2013.
- **Vedan Enterprise Corp / Vedan Vietnam** - minor: the 1954 MSG start belongs to the predecessor Ve Cheng Food per Vedan's own history page, and the 3,719 staff figure is the Vietnamese subsidiaries' share of 3,930 group staff at end-June 2024.
- **Tunas Baru Lampung** - dropped the Budi Delta Swakarya (30.05 percent) and Sungai Budi (28.63 percent) shareholdings: the only source is an undated third-party profile. Kontan confirms Sungai Budi Group ownership and the biodiesel, sugar and Rose Brand cooking oil lines.
- **PT Bio Inti Agrindo** - the POSCO page names the company only as "PT.BIA" and does not mention Daewoo International or any percentage; the 85 percent (2011) and POSCO's 60.3 percent of Daewoo (2013) come from the Norwegian Council on Ethics recommendation, which also records a 32,500 ha concession against the 34,195 ha of land use rights on the POSCO page. Both figures are now given with their dates.
- **Thor Chemical Holdings / Guernica** - removed "took in mercury-bearing waste from chemical companies in South Africa and abroad", which is not in the page opened. The entry now describes Thor SA (operator, factory closed by government in 1991, group demerger) and Guernica Chemicals Limited (SA-based, approached by Tato Holdings in 2019) and claims nothing about a "Thor Chemical Holdings", which no source opened names.
- **PowerChina Resources / Sinohydro** - removed "more than 10,000 employees and 524 international projects in over 74 countries worth nearly US$42.5 billion": those sit in an unattributed, undated third-party directory profile. The entry keeps the early-1950s founding, the Beijing office and the brand relationship from that profile, and anchors the 2011 merger and the wholly state-owned status of PowerChina in two government-published pages. Nothing is said about "PowerChina Resources", which no source opened names.
- **Rimbunan Hijau** - "who came from Sibu, Sarawak" removed: Free Malaysia Today says Tiong Hiew King was born in Sarawak and says nothing about Sibu or about where his brothers came from. The Forbes US$820 million figure carries no year in the source, so none is given.
- **WTK Group** - "12 concessions tied to it exported about 1.3 million cubic metres" corrected: ACT NOW! attributes the 1.3 million cubic metres (about 14 percent of PNG log exports, 2019-2021) to companies in the cluster, of which at least nine are logging companies and seven exported in that period. The managing director's name was completed as the source gives it.
- **CEZ Group** - unchanged in substance; the "About us" page was re-opened to confirm the 33,000 direct employees and 3.5 million customers, and the shareholder page confirms 69.78 percent of stated capital held by the Ministry of Finance at 31 December 2025.

## Weakest entries I let stand, and why

- **Shanghai Xinmingyuan Automobile Parts Co Ltd** - a single 2012 China Daily report is the only page naming the firm. It states the sector ("auto parts manufacturer") and places the firm in the Kangqiao area of Pudong, Shanghai. The entry claims nothing but sector and city, which is what the source carries; there is no register entry or website to open.
- **Shanghai Kangshuo Co Ltd** - same single source. It gives the full name, the recycling business and the relocation, and no ownership or size, so none is claimed.
- **Tozzi Green** - one magazine feature, no filing or register entry. New Lines is a reported longform outlet and the facts kept are the ones it states directly, but nothing is corroborated by a second source, and the Ravenna headquarters on the card remains unconfirmed (the source says only "a town near Ravenna" for the founding and "northern Italy" for the base).
- **Sabi Star / Max Mind Investments** - rests on one research tracker (Africa Mining Tracker, China Global South Project) rather than a filing; the Chengxin stake, price and date are not corroborated by a Shenzhen filing I could open.
- **Twigg Exploration and Mining Limitada** - the DFC loan and project cost come from a third-party project database (Early Warning System), not from DFC itself (disclosures.ifc.org and comparable pages are blocked); the ownership and the Balama operation are corroborated by Syrah's own annual report.
- **Tunas Baru Lampung** and **PT Intraco Penta** - the IDX listing dates and tickers rest on third-party company profiles; the business lines, group ownership and 2024 figures are corroborated by Indonesian business news outlets.
- **PT Permata Hijau Pasaman I** - kept rather than blanked only because an ISPO notice documents "unit 1" as an estate of PT Permata Hijau Pasaman. If the map's key means a separate company, nothing here describes it.
- **PowerChina Resources / Sinohydro** and **Thor Chemical Holdings / Guernica** - each key names two entities and only one is named in the sources. Each entry describes that one and says so in its note.
