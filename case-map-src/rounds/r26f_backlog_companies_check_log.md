# Fact-check log — comp_out.json (round 16, 6 company descriptions + 5 discrepancies)

Checked 2026-11-12 against COMP.md. Every URL cited by the writer was opened and read
directly; nothing below rests on the writer's own notes.

Outcome: 1 kept, 4 corrected, 1 blanked. Discrepancies: 3 confirmed, 2 rejected.

## Source qualification

The writer's claim to have used no market-data aggregators holds. The cited pages are:
IBBI (regulator), an NCLT order text, bogota.gov.co and metrodebogota.gov.co (city
government), NSE filing archives (BRSR and annual report), and four news outlets
(Kurir, Danas, The Nation Thailand, Kaohoon International). No StockAnalysis,
Screener.in, Tofler or sponsored trade-site page appears, and no Wikipedia page is
cited as a source.

One source is weaker than its label suggests: courtkutchehry.com, cited as the NCLT
Kochi order. It does carry the operative order text (bench, parties, admission,
default amount, moratorium) and I read those passages directly, but the page is partly
paywalled and the visible numbering stops short of the full order. Kept, flagged in
the entry's note.

## Discrepancies

Three confirmed, carried into comp_checked.json with case id, card wording, source
wording, URL and recommended replacement text. Two rejected.

1. CONFIRMED — `bogota-metro-line1-predios`, ML1 owners. The card's "Its owners are not
   named in any document I opened" is wrong: three Bogotá government documents name them,
   with the 85/15 split in all three and the minority holder's former and current names
   reconciled by the 2019 management report. The card must be corrected. This was the most
   serious of the five and it stands.
2. CONFIRMED, with the source attribution fixed — same case, signing date and scope. The
   27 November 2019 date does come from the Informe de gestión 2019. The wider scope does
   not: that report's own scope wording ("estudios y diseños de detalle, construcción,
   operación y mantenimiento") is no wider than the card's. The wider scope — "inversión,
   financiamiento, diseño, construcción, suministro, puesta en marcha, operación y
   mantenimiento" — is on the 2023 bogota.gov.co page. The writer's entry attributed both
   to the 2019 report. Confirmed on the facts, corrected on which document says what.
3. CONFIRMED — `kolkata-keiip-tranche2-vendors`, Furnace Fabrica. Insolvency since
   1 November 2023 and non-operational in March 2024, both read in the primary documents.
   The 2017 award is not contradicted, but the card presents a company that is now in CIRP
   and not trading as an ordinary contractor. Replacement wording supplied, dated rather
   than overwriting.

4. REJECTED as a discrepancy — `nis-railway-bypass-min-holding-tenants`. The writer's own
   text says Kurir is "consistent with the card". It is: the name, the limited-liability
   form and the 2015 bankruptcy all match, and the 2024 auction of a 384 m² building in the
   MIN complex does not speak to who owns the block of flats in the case, so it does not
   contradict "the report does not say who owns the building now" either. A card that the
   sources confirm is not a discrepancy; the array is for contradictions. The findings are
   recorded in this log and in the company's note instead, so nothing is lost.
5. REJECTED as a discrepancy — `kolkata-keiip-tranche2-vendors`, Simplex. The facts are
   confirmed (MRA 15 January 2025, 99.55%, no NCLT application admitted as at 31 March
   2026), but they do not make the card wrong: Simplex still exists, still trades, reported
   Rs 6,702 mns of standalone revenue and 561 employees, and the 2016 award stands. A debt
   restructuring in a going concern is not the kind of change — ownership, existence,
   insolvency — the card would mislead by omitting. Unlike Furnace Fabrica, where the
   company is non-operational and in CIRP. The restructuring is carried in the company
   description, which is where it belongs.

## Patterns worth carrying forward

- The "no blanks" warning was right, and in the one blank the fault was specific: a sector
  read off the company's own registered name. Worth a standing test — if removing the
  company's name from its description would leave the sector claim with no support, the
  claim is a restatement of the name.
- Twice the writer treated a figure belonging to a project as the company's own, or nearly
  did: ML1's 13.83 trillion peso project budget (cut) and the Furnace Fabrica default
  amount (correctly dated to 15.05.2023, not to the admission). Concession and
  special-purpose companies are where this fault concentrates, because the only numbers
  published about them are the project's.
- Two filings in this batch contradict themselves internally — Ramky on turnover and on
  the employee figure's date, Simplex on the 99.55% base and the project count. Checking a
  figure against a second mention inside the same document caught three errors here that
  checking it against the source list would not have.
- The writer twice declared a conflict ("the two city documents differ") without looking
  for a reconciliation in the documents it had already cited. Where sources disagree on a
  name, the check should be whether one of them records a renaming before the disagreement
  is printed as unresolved.
