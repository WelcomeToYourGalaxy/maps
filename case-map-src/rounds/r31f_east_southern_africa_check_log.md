## 1. The eight deaths, each tested separately

The test applied is the one in the brief, in two parts: which credit paid for the works decides whether a
death belongs to the project; whether a cause is stated decides whether it is counted. A settlement or
insurance payment is not an admission of causation and was treated as no evidence of it.

### `beira-maputo-climate-works-deaths-compensation` — `killed: 5` **upheld**

The completion report of 30 March 2021 (cover page: *Cities and Climate Change Project*, Mozambique,
March 30, 2021) carries the five in two footnotes, which the pre-extracted brief did not surface.

Footnote 30, on the Beira drainage works, paragraph 66: "These included: a worker hit by a third-party
vehicle while moving from one work site to another; 9-year old child that drowned in the retention basin
construction site during off-work hours; and two fatalities caused by traffic accidents involving
contractor's vehicles and pedestrians."

1. The worker hit by a third-party vehicle — cause stated, and he was moving between the project's work
   sites. The report counts him as "the fatality of one worker" under its own heading on non-compliance
   with the works' health and safety plans. **Counted.**
2. The nine-year-old drowned in the retention basin construction site — cause stated, and the site is the
   project's. **Counted.**
3. and 4. Two killed in traffic accidents involving the contractor's vehicles — cause stated.
   **Counted.**

Footnote 31, on the green urban infrastructure works: "The Beira GUI works also had a fatality of a 7-year
old child who was hit by a contractor's vehicle."

5. Cause stated. On which credit paid: the green infrastructure works were financed by the Bank's own
   additional financing of US$15.75 million under Component 2 (paragraph 14), with KfW parallel financing
   of EUR 18 million; the Bank required the health and safety audit and ran the post-closing plan on those
   works. The death belongs to the project. **Counted.**

Two further deaths in the same footnotes are correctly left uncounted and correctly named on the card:
"Another child was found dead in the drainage canals (outside construction sites) with no clear relation to
the project activities", and "A body was found in the Chiveve river retention basin during construction
(potential trespasser), but it was not identified that there was a relation of the fatality with the
construction activities."

**Figure left unreconciled (three readings, two documents).** The completion report's paragraph 66 and its
two footnotes give five incidents. The Independent Evaluation Group's review of 27 August 2021 gives four
in two places — "There were four fatalities: a worker was hit by a third-party vehicle while moving from one
site to another; a 9-year old child drowned in the retention basin construction site during off-work hours;
and two pedestrian died in traffic accidents involving contractor's vehicles", and later "four fatalities:
one worker and three community members" — omitting the seven-year-old on the green infrastructure works
altogether. The completion report's own lessons paragraph 85 says "in Beira where four fatalities of
community members were caused by traffic accidents involving construction vehicles", where its footnotes
give three traffic deaths and four community members in total. The review's lesson 4 says "In Beira, four

readings are on the card's `killed_note`; none is reconciled here, because no document reconciles them.

## 10. The extend — shape, and what it added

its own.

## 13. Did the pre-extracted briefs help or mislead?

**They helped the reading and misled the attribution.** Fifteen rows were worked with no fetch from the
lender, and reading `.txt` is why the two findings that decided the most here were findable at all: the

   parents, instead of asserting a shared operation.

## Why no cut, and what would go first

Nothing in this batch failed the three tests or the forward rule outright. Each kept card has a relocation
count from an outcome document with a relocation verb attached, or a documented harm to identifiable people,
and most have both: 57 households rehoused with an outside newspaper confirming the handover; 117 households
resettled in project-built houses with two fatalities, one attributed by the report to the works' own safety
failure; five deaths with stated causes and an audit finding a compensation process unlawful; RWF 18,648,741
marked unpaid in the borrower's own annexes; 37 households relocated and 13,600 staff retrenched as a

What the check did instead was correct: four errors in `lenders[0].role` across three cards, three
selective quotations, one claim inverted by reading the wrong plan, one figure attributed to the wrong
document, two dates taken off a record rather than a cover, one duplicated clause in the extend, one missing
death in the extend, and one missing `id` that would have broken the merge.

If one entry has to go, it is **`rwanda-electricity-access-rukarara-relocation`**: a single document, which
is a review of a completion report nobody has reached, every figure in it quoted from that report's page 38,
and the relocation itself at a sub-project another lender's money paid for. The reasons for keeping it are
in section 7 and they are reasons, not certainties.

The entry to reopen first, if more of its record is ever disclosed, is the drop
**`ethiopia-urban-water-supply-sanitation`**: the completion report records compensation delays and
under-compensated livelihood losses, which is limb (b) language, and the only thing missing is a place.
