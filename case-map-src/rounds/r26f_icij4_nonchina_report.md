## 2. My two weakest entries

**`bukoba-airport-resettlement-arrears` (Tanzania, P055120) is the weakest by a distance.** The entire harm is "five resettlement cases remained pending at project closure", four of them awaiting court deliberations, plus a demolished school that was rebuilt first on land the local government gave. There is no count of people, nobody named, no amount, no statement of what the four court cases were about, and no independent source. It passes limb (b) only on compensation being unpaid for five identifiable cases. The one later fact — that the cases were decided and payment was being processed — is attributed to the project team, not a document. If the checker wants to cut one entry, this is it.

**`dar-brt-kariakoo-clearance` (Tanzania, P103633) is the second weakest.** The 106 families are a real count in a real Bank document, and the clearance while litigation was pending is a real fact, but everything rests on a single document: the completion report. Two searches for an independent account of the Kariakoo clearance found nothing; one promising TanzLII hit turned out to be an unrelated land dispute at Boko, Kinondoni. The report also says the compensation was placed in escrow and that most affected people had claimed it, which cuts against the case, and it does not say how many of the 106 had been paid when the bulldozers arrived.
