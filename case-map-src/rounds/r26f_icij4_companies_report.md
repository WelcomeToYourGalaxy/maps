# Round 20, ICIJ tranche 4 — entity descriptions, 27 names

16 written, 11 blank (41%). The blank rate is far above the usual 5–20% and that is the
material, not restraint for its own sake: 25 of the 27 names are government bodies, and
nine of the eleven blanks are Chinese provincial or municipal loan offices, bureaux and a
railway project company for which no government instrument, own portal or filing could be
opened. Every entry below says what was opened.

## 1. Name by name

### Written (16)

- **Nanchang Urban Rail Group Co.** — A credit rating report on 南昌轨道交通集团有限公司,
  filed as corporate bond disclosure on the Shanghai Stock Exchange's own server
  (static.sse.com.cn), gives 100% control by the Nanchang municipal state-asset commission,
  registered and paid-in capital of 26.15亿元 and consolidated assets of 724.95亿元 at end-March

  approval or staff.

  2021: the office dated from 1986, its sign came down that afternoon and the National
  Administration for Rural Revitalization was established at the same address. I did not write
  "standing office of the State Council leading group", because the only support for it is the
  words inside the body's own name.

  Tajikistan, generation/transmission/distribution, 11,230 employees at 31 December 2018. I used

  2,634,931 thousand somoni, a cash figure, not reported revenue.
- **Accra Metropolitan Assembly** — the Assembly's own "The Assembly" and "History" pages: Local
  Government Act 1993 (Act 462), since amended as the Local Governance Act 2016 (Act 936), L.I.
  2034, a General Assembly with subordinate Sub-Metropolitan District Councils. The pages' 2010

- **Direccion Provincial de Vialidad, Province of Santa Fe** — weakest-but-defensible pairing: a

- **Direccion Nacional de Vialidad** — Decree 461/2025, official text on argentina.gob.ar:
  constituted as an autarchic body by Decreto-Ley 505/58, latterly in the Secretaría de

- **Pimpri Chinchwad Municipal Corporation** — the CAG's Maharashtra local-bodies chapter:

- **Hubli-Dharwad Bus Rapid Transit System Company** — CAG Karnataka PSU report chapter 1 and

  Company Limited", paid-up capital ₹20 crore (2015-16 accounts). No administrative department

- **Tanzania National Roads Agency** — the Controller and Auditor General's performance audit on

- **Dar Rapid Transit Agency** — thin. The only text found anywhere stating its instrument is its

- **Kenya Urban Roads Authority** — KURA's own page: Kenya Roads Act 2007, national trunk roads

- **National Land Commission** — the Commission's own history page: Article 67(1) of the 2010

- **Tanzania Airports Authority** — the establishment order itself, G.N. 404 of 1999 on TanzLII:

## 2. My own two weakest entries

1. **Dar Rapid Transit Agency.** The whole entry rests on one line of self-description inside a
   tender notice republished by a tender aggregator. The instrument number is specific enough to
   be checkable and nothing else is claimed, but the host does not meet the spirit of COMP.md and
   a checker would be right to demand the Government Notice itself or a Tanzanian audit report.
2. **Direccion Provincial de Vialidad, Province of Santa Fe.** The ministry attachment is inferred
   from a budget line ("Jurisdicción: 62 - MOP y Vivienda, Entidad: 601 - Vialidad") and from a
   tender page that lists the Ministry of Public Works as the tender's organismo without saying the
   directorate belongs to it. Two weak supports pointing the same way, not one strong one.

   Honourable third: **Resettlement Research Center, Hohai University**, where the unit I describe
   may not be the unit the completion report meant.

## 3. Discrepancies — 14 across 8 cases

In `/home/claude/r20/r26f_icij4_companies_disc.json`, keyed by case id. Nothing was changed.
The substantive ones:

- **santa-fe-road-19-expropriation** — the DNV card speaks in the present tense of a body dissolved
  by decree on 7 July 2025; and the decree recites Decreto-Ley 505/58, not the 1932 law usually
  given as the DNV's creation.
- **kwabenya-landfill-expropriation** — the Ghanaian ministry on the card was merged out of
  existence in January 2025 under Executive Instrument 1.
- **rogun-stage1-resettlement** — the map's "Open Shareholding Holding Company Barki Tojik" is the
  Bank's rendering; the audited accounts say Open Joint Stock Holding Company "Barqi Tojik". An
  April 2018 decree split transmission and distribution into two new joint-stock companies, so
  "Tajikistan's state electricity company" is true for the case period and not for the structure
  since. And the "Poyry Energy Ltd" name cannot be tied to any corporate record.
- **nutrip-road-compensation-arrears** — KURA's own remit is national trunk roads *within urban
  areas*, not urban roads; the NLC's own page does not carry the valuation-and-payment role the
  card gives it, which wants a Land Act 2012 citation.
- **bukoba-airport-resettlement-arrears** — TAA operates government-owned airports; the card says
  it owns them.
- **nanchang-line-2-demolition**, **sutp-brts-corridor-displacement**, **zhanghu-railway-
  resettlement** — three naming flags (registered name vs report rendering; the live Nava Raipur
  rename; and the Huzhang/Zhanghu ordering), each left on the card per the round 17 precedent.

## 4. Notes for the next batch

- **A lender document's institutional history was not used anywhere here**, per the round 19
  caution. Where a card's institutional sentence had only the completion report behind it and no
  government instrument could be opened, the name was left blank rather than restated.
- **cag.gov.in now 302-redirects its PDFs to a CloudFront host, and the redirect cannot be
  followed in this environment** (`https://d1jmc0u14z2tmp.cloudfront.net/uploads/...` was refused
  for provenance). The same documents are served directly from `saiindia.gov.in/uploads/...`,
  which works — use that host for Indian audit reports.
- **Blocked or unusable in this pass:** `https://www.ncmtr.com/` (provenance refused, no search
  result to route through), `https://tanzlii.org/en/akn/tz/act/gn/2025/594/eng@2025-10-03`
  (client error), `thecitizen.co.tz` article body (did not load),
  `https://d1jmc0u14z2tmp.cloudfront.net/uploads/download_audit_report/2016/Annexures_Karnataka_PSUs_Report_No_3_2016.pdf`
  and the 2009 Maharashtra appendices on the same host.
- **Chinese official domains barely surface in search**, which is what drove the blank rate. What
  did work was the exchanges' own disclosure servers (static.sse.com.cn), NDRC and Xinhua. Chinese
  provincial department and county-office pages did not surface at all; Eastmoney-hosted filing
  PDFs do surface, but they are an aggregator host and the one opened was the wrong issuer.
