## Lead 20 — `nenskra-hydropower-chuberi-nakra-land` — CUT

**Opened:** CEE Bankwatch field report of 11 September 2017 (the single source for the harm);
Bankwatch blog of the same date; Bankwatch status update of 9 April 2020 and the Green
Alternative copy of it; EIB Complaints Mechanism conclusions report of 7 February 2020; EBRD
IPAM case page `ipam/2018/08.html`; EBRD IPAM case registry entry; Green Alternative and
Bankwatch NGO report to the Bern Convention of 12 February 2024 (`rm.coe.int`).

**Verdict: drop.** The conflict with the other agent resolves in the other agent's favour.

1. **The one source for the harm is written in the future tense.** The Bankwatch report says
   "PAPs in these areas **will be** mainly affected by the temporary or permanent loss of
   pastures", a household "**will be** permanently affected by loss of pasture land and a wooden
   cabin at Mashrichala", "who **will lose** access to the Kvemo Memuli pasture area", and
   households "**would lose** access" to cabins. The draft printed "Access to cabins **is
   lost**" and "the people affected **are** mainly affected by the … loss of pastures". That is
   the hedge-promotion CHECK16 §2 names as the single most likely fault, and it is what the
   case was built on.
2. **No land was ever taken from anyone.** The grant of 96.2 ha in Chuberi for USD 1 and
   600.0252 ha for 55 years is a transfer on paper to the project company. Nothing I opened
   records a person losing the use of that land, a tree felled or a household moved.
3. **No compensation was owed and withheld.** No agreements were signed — "This has not
   happened, but it is unclear what the new timeline is" — so there were offers outstanding for
   losses that had not occurred. The female-headed household at Kvemo Memuli "**has been offered
   no** fodder or monetary compensation", which is a gap in an offer, not a loss.
4. **The lender's own report carries nothing on the ground.** The EIB report of 7 February 2020
   does not state whether any land was transferred, acquired or occupied, and does not state
   whether compensation was paid, owed or withheld. The words LALRP, pastures, cabins and Kvemo
   Memuli do not appear in it. Its findings are findings against the Bank. I also corrected the
   draft's framing: the report does not "record no completed land take" — it is silent.
5. **Timing fails.** The 2024 Bern Convention report: "Local people informed us that work on
   none of the three projects has started yet" and "To the best of our knowledge, none has a
   construction company selected." Bankwatch, April 2020: the company abandoned the project
   "before construction had even begun" and "almost all preparatory works have been stopped".
   The IPAM Monitoring Closing Report of 24 June 2026: "IPAM has been informed by Management
   that the Project has been cancelled."

**The two figure pairs:** both reported accurately in the draft and neither side dropped. Left

## Lead 22 — `mzuzu-nrwb-water-efficiency-house-damage` — CUT

**Opened:** EIB Complaints Mechanism final monitoring report for Chipambo and Geisha of
15 December 2022; second monitoring report for Kazando of 15 March 2024.

**Verdict: drop. The causation is cut from under it, as the task suspected.**

- The question asked — is a payment for assessed damage the promoter accepting causation, or
  settling without admission? — answers against the case. The report describes the promoter's
  approach as "to assess the damage to structures of the households concerned, evaluate the
  cost and then make the payment", and the payments as made under its **"standing offer to
  pay"**, with no causal finding attached. The Kazando report likewise never attributes the
  damage to the works; its only wording is "the damage to structures" and "the damaged
  structures in question".
- In the one strand actually tested, the jointly chosen independent engineer found it "cannot
  be established" that the excavation caused the damage, and both parties accepted that. So the
  only engineering test performed found **against** causation.
- **Neither report says any land was taken or occupied by the works.** They record valuations
  and payments. The draft's lever text, "234 households … were valued for land taken for the
  works", is the writer's inference. Compensation that was late and then paid is not documented
  harm — the writer dropped the Tunisian G1 Pénétrante Sud lead on exactly that ground.

**Two sourcing errors found by reading the reports:**
1. The collapsed house. The report's sentence is "One refused to receive payment because
   **allegedly** his house collapsed." That is the office's own wording with a hedge in it. The
   draft quoted the clause with "allegedly" removed and described it as the household's own
   statement. The task asked whether the case text promoted it into a fact the office found — it
   did the reverse of what was needed: it stripped the office's hedge.
2. The Kazando counts. The draft said "The office's second monitoring report **says** the
   household counts of 13, 14, 15 and 17 differ across its own sections and does not reconcile
   them." The report says no such thing. It simply uses the counts — "the remaining compensation
   to 13 households", "the draft valuation report for the 14 missing households", "the Promoter
   made the payments to 15 households" with "Two households had already been paid previously",
   "paid compensation to the 17 affected members of the community" — without reconciling them.
   The observation is the writer's own, attributed to the office. **The counts themselves are
   quoted accurately and the lack of reconciliation is real** — that part of the task's check
   passes — and there is a further unreconciled pair the draft missed, between "two additional
   communal water points" in action 4 and a "request for additional three communal water points"
   in its status note.

Everything else I checked was accurate: the 20 of 38 paid on 13 December 2022, the assessment
of 23 September–3 October 2022 disclosed 19 October 2022, the 11 objections over cracks "not
included in the assessment of the damage", 17 members paid 4 August 2022, 15 households paid
26 August 2022, the MWK 47m estimate, the 22 connections as the promoter's own figure, and the
EIB's EUR 24.6m approved November 2016 against a EUR 60.4m total cost. No amounts paid are
given anywhere.
