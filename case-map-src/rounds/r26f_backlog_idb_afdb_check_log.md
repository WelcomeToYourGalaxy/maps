3. **The monitoring report's own water finding runs against the case, and the draft omitted it.** At action 3.3 the second and third monitoring findings both say: *"Water quality appears not to have been affected by the construction of the Medupi Plant. ## Patterns worth carrying forward

1. **A hedge in a monitoring-table cell is still a hedge, and a phrase lifted out of one loses the sentence that qualified it.** The whole Medupi case rested on five words — "damage done to Mokolo River" — pulled out of a sentence that attributed that damage to *previous* sand mining and whose actual finding was that no independent assessment had been done. Checking the column header and the rest of the row killed the case.
2. **When a draft quotes a document selectively, check the same document for the sentence that cuts the other way.** The Medupi monitoring report's own water-quality finding — "appears not to have been affected by the construction of the Medupi Plant" — sat two rows below the quote the case was built on and was not mentioned.
3. **"The report does not explain the gap" is an absence claim and failed again.** Both times here it was checkable in minutes with a grep, and one of the two was wrong. Treat every such sentence as a task, not a statement.
4. **Cloudflare 403s are being read as source availability.** Four of the Polihali figures, including the headline loan amount, came from Creamer Media pages that return HTTP 403 behind a bot challenge. The lender's own appraisal report was sitting on `ewsdata.rightsindevelopment.org` the whole time and gave better figures, in its own currency, with resettlement counts the news reports did not have. Reach for the development bank's appraisal report before the trade press.
5. **A number in a news headline can be an unrelated percentage from the loan document.** US$86.72 million versus the Government of South Africa's 86.2 per cent cost share is close enough to be worth a second look whenever a dollar figure has suspicious precision.
6. **Checking a weak case can make it stronger.** The Aché entry gained a documented harm strand — several Aché leaders jailed in 2006 after resisting a logger invasion, with IDB project funds paying the lawyer who got them out — and an unhedged Panel sentence about "harm now experienced" that the draft had replaced with the forward-looking one. Reading for what the writer *missed* matters as much as reading for what it overstated.
