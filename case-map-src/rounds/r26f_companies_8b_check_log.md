## Weakest entries let stand, and why

- **Zhejiang Kaishan Compressor** and **TBEA Co.** — supported only by market-data aggregators (StockAnalysis, Simply Wall St), which are not filings, company pages or news outlets. Left standing because the facts kept are narrow (name, listing, employees, revenue with its stated fiscal year) and nothing in them is contradicted; TBEA's founding year was dropped precisely because the two aggregators disagree.
- **Zuari Agro Chemicals** — Screener.in only, a filings aggregator. The content matches what Zuari's own disclosures are known to say, but no company report or exchange filing was opened.
- **Royal Group** — a single trade-directory page (IPP Journal) for the founding year, the leader, the address and the sector list. No company site or register entry was found.
- **Perenco Guatemala Limited** — a single BNamericas profile; the "Anglo-French" parent description is that page's characterisation rather than a Perenco statement.
- **Rosatom / Atomstroyexport** — an NGO campaigning report (Ecodefense, hosted by a German citizens' initiative). The figures are attributed in it to Rosatom's own reporting and the report's internal caveats were added, but no Rosatom document was opened.
- **Poly Hong Dong Pelagic Fishery Co.** — the exact name in the key appears on no page opened; the entry rests on one aggregated Outlaw Ocean plant record that itself cites other outlets for the two main claims.
- **Santo Antonio Energia** — two credible sources disagree on the shareholder percentages and on which company is the parent. The dated 2022 figures are used in the desc and the conflict is recorded rather than resolved.
- **Sinohydro Corporation (PowerChina)** — the only page opened describes PowerChina, not Sinohydro. Kept because the key names both and the text now says plainly which entity the facts belong to.
