## The writer's named weak call: the Zambian promotion and the republication

**The promotion stands.** The republication is admissible, but not as the thing the writer called it,
and the card now says so.

service workers, Magande's televised assurance a week earlier that he would not raise taxes, his

October request to relax the ceiling, and the September Copperbelt strike. I accepted SADOCC for
these on three grounds: it names the paper, the city and the date for each item; the items are

cent tax figures, same PRGF framing, same missed December 2003 completion point), which is the only
fidelity check available and it passes; and the card's 90 per cent claim was already carried by IRIN,
a source the card held before this round. One web search found no copy of any of the four Times items

Three things had to change in the entry:

1. **The writer undercounted its own reliance.** The confidence_note said two of three Times of
   Zambia items were read in republication. There are four Zambian press items in the entry — Times

   19 February — and all four, plus both IRIN items, were read in third-party copies. Not one is on a
   publisher's own page. The note now says that, and puts the promotion's weight where it belongs.
2. **The 9,000 and 12,000 are not the minister's figures.** In the 20 October text the minister is
   quoted on needing more teachers, on having to follow IMF policies to reach the completion point and

   newspaper's own voice — and that sentence also attributes the ceiling to the IMF, in the paper's
   voice, which the writer dropped. The writer's report called them figures "from the responsible
   minister in the national press"; they are the paper's. The card text already said "the same report
   gives"; it now records the voice and the paper's own causal attribution.
3. **`documented` must not be read as settling the cause.** The note now states that the rating covers

## 4. salem-tnudp-squatter-resettlement

**The discard was right, and is now on firmer ground.** The completion report's CMDA annex says: "Of
the 23 road sub-projects initially identified, 5 road sub-projects covering 34.80 km were upgraded
under this component (the balance sub-projects have been dropped during mid-term review of TNUDP III
and subsequently taken up with State funds)." That sentence — which the writer did not quote — puts
the dropped corridors outside Bank financing explicitly. The dates are a second, independent reason:
every award and government order in the discarded litigation post-dates the August 2009 restructuring
(Porur Award No.3/2011 of 15 February 2011; the Arumbakkam orders of 24 June 2011 and 2 November 2012;
East Coast Road Award No.4/2015 of 4 December 2015). Both added. I also opened the Anandaraj order
myself and the writer's reading of it is accurate in every detail.

What the discard does not do, and the entry now says so: the report never says which corridors the 212
title holders and 337 non-title holders were on. The five-road list rules the litigation out of Bank
financing; it does not positively place the Bank-financed acquisitions.

**The Salem riverbank inference is cut back, because its premise is false.** The writer's basis was
"the one Salem sub-project in the project's own list is the river improvement scheme". The report's
line-of-credit passage says "Salem dominates the list with 6 projects". Salem had six TNUDP-III
sub-projects; the river improvement is the only one the report names, and the beneficiary-survey table
the writer read lists the 17 sub-projects visited for that survey, not the project's sub-projects. So
the report establishes that resettlement happened in one of 110 sub-projects and that the town was
Salem, and nothing more. The river works remain the likeliest of the six and the entry now says that —
a hypothesis for a next round, not an inference, and not for the card. The writer was right to flag it
as an inference; it is weaker than the flag suggested.

Also noted: the report counts the Chennai transport proposals twice, 21 in section 1.7 and 23 in the
CMDA annex. Everything else I checked holds — the US$147.5 million to US$34 million cut, the
