## What I could not check, and what stopped me

Nothing was left unchecked for want of a document: every one of the 20 cases had its ICR and ICRR
in the cache, and the three I needed an appraisal document for had that too. Two limits worth
recording:

- **The Qingyang journey-time row.** The report calls the end target 'substantially achieved' while
  the actual (15.80) beats the target (18.20) by the 13.2 per cent its own annex computes. The
  label and the arithmetic do not agree, and nothing in the document explains the word
  'substantially'. The paragraph now gives the three values, the report's word, and the annex's
  13.2 per cent, and draws no conclusion.
- **Ethiopia-Sudan's US$8.76 million and 175.31 GWh** are the only outcome figures in that
  framework, and both are dated 30 June 2013 against a target date of 31 December 2011, so they
  cover a different period from the target they are set against. The report says as much (an export
  period of five months against the two years assumed) and the paragraph carries that.
- **The whole batch has no source outside the Bank Group or the borrower**, so nothing in any of
  the 20 paragraphs could be cross-checked against an outside figure. That is the reason
  `self_reported` is true on all 20, and each paragraph says so.
