# Whole-picture text, round 22 batch (`rounds/r26f_icij6.json`) — writer's report

21 paragraphs written, none blank. **Zero web searches**: every figure comes from the paired
`.pdf`/`.txt` documents already in `/home/claude/r22/cache`. Each case had both a completion report
(ICR) and an Evaluation Group review (ICRR) in the cache except two, noted below.

`self_reported` is **true on all 21**. Every document used is a World Bank Group product — the
Bank's own completion report, the Independent Evaluation Group's review of it, or (two cases) the
Group's Project Performance Assessment Report. The Evaluation Group sits inside the World Bank
Group and is therefore not an independent check; nor is a survey commissioned and paid for under
the project, of which three turned up (Xuancheng's Project Social Economic Impact Survey, the small
towns' social and economic impact survey agreed as a remedial measure, and Kampala's Citizens
Report Card surveys, whose provenance the review does not state — each is flagged in the paragraph
or in `why`).

## Method, applied to every case
- The **results framework annex was read row by row**, including every provincial, lot or
  utility-level sub-row, not the report's summary of it. Adverse rows are in the paragraph wherever
  they exist.
- Where a target was changed, **both the appraisal target and the revised one are given**, or the
  paragraph says the indicator or target was created at a later restructuring.
- **Direction checked** on every indicator before any number was called a shortfall. Nine
  indicators in this batch count downward (referrals, length of stay, travel time, transit time,
  vehicle operating costs, fatality rates, losses, roughness, incidents) and none of them is
  described as a shortfall when it fell.
- **Both documents' efficiency and relevance ratings** are taken from the document that gave them,
  out of the ICR's own body text and the review's own rating line, because the newer review
  template has no efficiency or relevance row.

## My own two weakest paragraphs

**1. `maluku-sez-maes-inkiene-demolition`.** The three production-volume rows are the weakest
figures in the batch and I could not resolve them. The framework table reads 1,325.86, 35.4 and
113,656 tonnes; the report's comments on the same rows read 1,325,860, 35,400 and 113,656,000 tons;
and the palm oil row is 25,257 per cent of its stated target on the report's own arithmetic, which
no plausible unit reading fixes. I put the table's figures in the paragraph and said the units
cannot be relied on. A checker should decide whether to keep them at all or cut the three volume
rows and leave the yields, the roads, the jobs and the zone outputs, which are clean. The second
soft spot in the same paragraph is "some 300 land concession holders were compensated" and "a
resettlement action plan was fully implemented": that is the completion report's wording about the
very thing this case says went wrong, and the disbursement suspension over resettlement safeguards
is in the paragraph two sentences later. I have kept both and attributed the first to the report,
but a checker may want the tension made explicit rather than left to the reader.

**2. `wuhan-middle-ring-resettlement-overrun`.** The whole paragraph rests on one document, the
Evaluation Group's review, with no completion report in the cache to check it against, and that
document is visibly unreliable in places — the Wuchang fatality sentence contradicts its own
figures, and a front-end fee of US$1 million in the review sits against US$2 million in the
appraisal document. I reported the fatality sentence as printed and left the figure out of use, and
gave the three travel-time measurements in the review's relative terms ("one minute better than

least checkable paragraph here and the one most likely to need a fetch of the Wuhan completion
