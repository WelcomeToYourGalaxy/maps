## The three tests (a case must pass all three, or be marked "contested" with the reason, or be dropped)
1. **Link.** The World Bank Group or IMF tie must be documented, not just alleged: an IMF letter of intent, staff report, press release or program document naming the measure; a World Bank project or policy-loan document; or an independent second source (established press, peer-reviewed study, court, UN body) stating the measure was a loan condition or financed by the loan. Protest news that merely mentions the IMF is not enough on its own. If the link rests only on a government's own statement or on critics, mark "contested" and say so in confidence_note.
2. **Harm.** Actual harm on the ground, with figures where they exist: price rises with numbers, lost homes, land or jobs, deaths, injuries, arrests, health effects, environmental damage. Expected or feared harm does not count.
3. **Timing.** The lender's program or project must have been in force (or the measure agreed with it) when the harm happened. If the program came after, drop the case.

## JSON shape (one object per case, same keys as the existing cases)
id (kebab-case, unique), title, place, iso3, lat, lng (the specific spot, e.g. where the protest or deaths happened, or the project site), point (what the point marks), start, end (years; end null if ongoing), mechanism (one of: privatization, austerity, project, land, trade), lenders [{name, role}] (name like "IMF", "World Bank (IBRD/IDA)", "IFC", "MIGA", "ICSID (World Bank Group)", or a regional development bank only as a co-lender), conditions (what the loan required or financed, with the document), companies [{name, role, hq, hq_lat, hq_lng}] (only if documented; else []), community (the harm, with figures and who reported them), suppression (police or army response, arrests; "" if none), killed (integer, 0 if none; count only deaths sources tie to the episode), killed_note, outcome, sources [{title, publisher, year, url}] (every page relied on), confidence ("documented" or "contested"), confidence_note (what is uncertain or disputed, including the lender's or government's denial where there is one), lk (lender keys from: wb, imf, ifc, miga, icsid, reg), cp ([] unless a World Bank Inspection Panel "ip" or CAO "cao" complaint exists), rg (one of: "Africa south of the Sahara", "Latin America and the Caribbean", "East Asia and the Pacific", "South Asia", "Europe and Central Asia", "Middle East and North Africa"), cx [], levers (list from: deval, prices, cuts, tax, priv, trade, land, fees, labour, project, finance), lvwhy ({lever: one short sentence on how this case used it}).

## Writing
Plain words a novice can follow. No opinion words, no "brutal", no "shocking". Give the figure, not a characterization. Credit figures to who reported them, e.g. "(Reuters)". Include the lender's or government's own account where it differs. Never write "I", "brief", "the task" or "sources opened" in any text field. Short quotes only (under 15 words), at most one per source.
