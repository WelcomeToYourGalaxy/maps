## What to decide

Apply the forward rule in the handoff, item 9 of "What is actually open": a case needs either (a) a document counting people or households actually **relocated**, or (b) a specific documented harm to identifiable people — deaths with a stated cause, injuries, arrests, land taken, compensation unpaid or below replacement cost, livelihoods not restored. Neither limb is a drop, and a card whose entire content is a count of people "affected" is a drop. Where a document records housing actually demolished, in a table with a separate enterprise column, limb (b) is met without a household count.
